# Cardinal Care Bridge — brand notes

## The line

> **A place Lou would call home.**

## Where it comes from

Lou Brizzi. Rob's dad. The man who inspired *The Cardinal's Promise*.

He raised Rob in New Jersey. He's the reason the cardinal means what it
means. He died on Christmas Day, 2025. The cardinal came on January 9th —
Rob's sobriety anniversary, fifteen days later.

The memoir, the mission, and this business all trace back to him: sit in
the rooms most people leave. Say the unspoken words. Think of what's best
and most comfortable for the person at the end of life.

Lou is the standard. Every recommendation this business makes gets measured
against one question: *would Lou call this place home?*

That's not a slogan. It's the actual test. A community either passes it or
it doesn't.

## How to use it

- As the measure behind "We don't start with communities. We start with
  people." — Lou is the person.
- On the site, in the guide, in conversation with families: it works
  because every family has their own Lou. The line hands them the test.
- Say it plainly. Don't explain it. Don't decorate it.

## How not to use it

- Never as a hook for urgency or fear.
- Never paired with discounts, promotions, or sales pressure.
- Never abstracted into "our founder's father" language. His name was Lou.
- If a piece of marketing wouldn't be worthy of him, the line doesn't go
  on it — and neither should the piece run.

## Status

Noted for later use — not yet placed on the live page. When it goes on,
it goes where the weight belongs: near the promise, not near the buttons.
