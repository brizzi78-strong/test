What Happens When You Call

Five steps, start to settled

No obligation at any point, and you can stop at any step.

1

We listen first

A conversation about your loved one — health needs, budget, location, routines, what matters to them. No forms before we talk.

2

Our nurse assesses the level of care

A clinical read on what setting actually fits. Getting this wrong is the most expensive mistake families make — and most referral services never look closely enough to get it right.

3

We shortlist real options

Communities we have visited, with current availability and pricing — matched to your loved one's needs and budget, not to whoever pays us most.

4

We tour with you

We schedule the visits and go with you, so someone in the room knows what to ask and what to look for.

5

We help you land

Paperwork, clinical documentation, move logistics — and check-ins through the first month, when families need us most.

What This Costs Your Family

Nothing.

Our guidance is free to families — the consultation, the assessment, the shortlist, the tours, and the follow-up after move-in.

We are paid a referral fee by the community your loved one chooses, and only if a move actually happens. That is how placement advisory works across the industry, and we would rather you hear it plainly from us than wonder about it later.

Two commitments that follow from it: we will tell you when a community is not right even though a placement would pay us, and we will tell you if the honest answer is that your loved one should stay home for now.
