A family guide

Family care homes in the Triangle and nearby counties

Family care homes are the smallest licensed senior living option in North Carolina — a regular house, licensed by the state for two to six residents, run by a small staff who are there around the clock. For many families they are the closest thing to home that still comes with care, and most of them are nearly impossible to find online. This is the list we wish had existed.

Before you visit any home on this list

A listing here is not an endorsement. Three things to check yourself, all free:

  1. Confirm the license. The NC Division of Health Service Regulation publishes the current list of every licensed family care home and adult care home. Search it at info.ncdhhs.gov/dhsr/acls/faclistings.html. If a home isn't on it, don't move anyone in.
  2. Read the star rating and inspection history. Every licensed home has a state star-rated certificate and a public inspection record on the same DHSR site. Ask the home to show you the certificate on the wall, too.
  3. Call before you drive. Addresses here come from public listings and state records, and small homes move, rename, and change hands. Confirm the address, confirm there's an opening, and ask who will be on shift when you visit.
