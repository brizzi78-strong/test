# Free listings & posts — copy-paste kit

Ready-to-paste text for every free online channel. Nothing here needs a
designer or a budget — just an account and ten minutes per channel. Keep the
wording consistent everywhere: same name, same phone, same service area.
Search engines and families both reward consistency.

**The one rule:** every channel gets its own "how did you hear about us"
tag (listed with each section) so leads can be attributed per
[`../../LEAD_GENERATION_STRATEGY.md`](../../LEAD_GENERATION_STRATEGY.md).

---

## 4. Community resource lists (the trust-transfer channel)

These are directories families actually get handed by social workers. All
free; each takes an email or a phone call. **Tracking tag:** "community
directory" (or the specific org's name once volume justifies it).

| Where | How |
| --- | --- |
| Triangle J Area Agency on Aging ([tjcog.org](https://www.tjcog.org)) | Ask how local service providers get included in their resource and referral lists; their senior centers and events calendar is also where the print flyer belongs. |
| NC 211 ([nc211.org](https://nc211.org)) | United Way's statewide database — ask about adding the service to their directory; social workers search it daily. |
| County senior centers (Wake, Durham, Orange…) | Bring `flyer.html` printed, ask the front desk about the bulletin board and their resource binder. |
| Local libraries, YMCAs, churches | Community boards — same flyer, always ask first. |

**Short directory blurb (many forms cap length — 240 characters):**

> Cardinal Care Bridge — free senior living advisory for Triangle
> families. Compare Independent/Assisted Living & Memory Care, tour and
> move coordination, support after move-in. Wake–Durham–Orange & beyond.
> (850) 221-7443.

## What deliberately isn't in this kit

- **The Lou line.** "A place Lou would call home" stays on the site and in
  conversation, per [`../BRAND.md`](../BRAND.md) — it doesn't go on posts
  and listings that end in an ask.
- **Urgency or fear.** No "before it's too late," no crisis countdowns.
  If a post needs fear to work, it doesn't run.
- **Paid anything.** Aggregators and Google Ads have their own entries in
  the lead-gen strategy, with conditions. This kit is the free layer.
