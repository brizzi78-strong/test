For discharge planners & case managers

A patient can't safely return home. We take it from there.

We help the family compare Independent Living, Assisted Living, and Memory Care options, check real availability, coordinate tours and the move, and stay with them after move-in. No cost to your team or the family.

STEP 1

Call or text us

Same-day response, including Fridays and weekends. If we can't take the case, we say so on that call — not two days later.

STEP 2

We talk to the family

Care needs, budget, payer, geography, and what matters to the person moving. Then a short list of communities with confirmed current availability.

STEP 3

We close the loop

Tours, paperwork, move coordination — and we tell you where the patient landed, so your file is complete.

Helpful to have when you call (none required)

  • Family decision-maker's name and phone number
  • Level of care being discharged to (IL / AL / Memory Care)
  • Mobility and cognition notes relevant to placement
  • Payer picture: private pay, LTC insurance, VA, Medicaid pending
  • Target discharge date

What we don't do

  • No pressure on families, ever
  • No steering to a favored facility — the short list fits the person
  • No fee to the hospital, the family, or your team
  • We're paid a standard referral fee by the community a family chooses, and we tell families that plainly
