Pairs with [`graphics.html`](graphics.html) (three screenshot-ready 1080×1080
posts) and extends [`../listings.md`](../listings.md), which covers the
one-time Nextdoor/Facebook *introduction* posts. This file is the ongoing
content calendar: captions to post on the business pages themselves, plus
one for the professional-network channel that isn't a listing at all.

the Lou line stays off anything ending in an ask, and the referral-fee
model gets said plainly wherever the offer is free. Each post gets its own
**tracking tag** so replies and DMs can be attributed per

## Posting cadence (a starting point, not a rule)

Three posts a week is enough to keep the pages active without turning this
into a second job: one offer/guide post, one quote or "why us" post, one
practical/informational post (services, a caregiver tip, a local resource
like TJCOG or NC 211). Reuse the three graphics on a loop before making
new ones — the point is consistency, not volume.
