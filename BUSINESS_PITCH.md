# Cardinal Care Bridge — the pitch

One page. The version you say out loud is in **bold** at each step.

---

## The problem

**Every week in the Triangle, a hospital tells a family their mother can't go
home — and gives them 72 hours to figure out the rest.**

What exists to help them is a national lead mill: the family's phone number is
sold to five communities at once, a call-center advisor who has never seen the
buildings reads from a database, and the phone doesn't stop ringing for days.
Communities pay 75–100% of the first month's rent for those cold leads —
because there's nothing better.

## The answer

**Cardinal Care Bridge: local placement by people who have walked every
building they recommend. Free to families. Communities pay — the same fee they
already pay the lead mills, for referrals that actually convert.**

- We meet families in person and tour with them
- We answer in one hour, always — a discharge planner on a 72-hour clock uses
  whoever answers
- We recommend two or three right-fit communities, not a spray of five
- Every recommendation passes one test: **would we place our own father there?**
  The founder's father is why the company exists.

## Why we win

| Asset | What it does |

| **The book** — *The Cardinal's Promise* | "He wrote the book on this" — cheapest credibility in the industry |
| Certified Dementia Practitioner on the team | Memory care is the highest-fee, highest-stakes placement — we're credentialed for it |

| **Private duty partnership** (in motion) | In-home aides see "it's time" weeks before any hospital does — the earliest, warmest placement lead that exists, plus a two-way flow: our not-ready families become their clients |

## The model

- **Fee:** 50–100% of first month's rent, paid by the community. Planning
  number **$4,500/placement** (Triangle AL ~$6,350/mo, memory care ~$7,036/mo)
- **Cost structure:** near zero — no inventory, no payroll at launch,
  commission-only growth
- **Year-one revenue:** ~$135K conservative / ~$235K base / ~$390K strong
- **The honest hard part:** 2–4 months from first inquiry to first collected
  check. Runway, not leads, is what kills placement agencies.

## The roadmap

1. **Days 1–90:** 25+ signed community agreements (supply first — signed
   *before* partnership conversations), the 90-day private duty referral
   pilot, 20 discharge-planner introductions, 1–3 placements
2. **Months 4–6:** 2–4 placements/month, renegotiate early agreements to 100%
   on track record, book in every referrer's hands
3. **Months 7–12:** 4–8/month, own the Triangle
4. **Territory 2: South Florida** — a trusted partner (CDP®) is already on the
   ground; proven playbook travels, cold starts don't
5. **Later, by evidence only:** more territories via affiliates, a second
   advisor, the integrated private-duty stack, or the software — each gated on
   data, not enthusiasm

## The guardrails (they're also the brand)

- Communities pay our fee. Families never do. Said early and unprompted.
- **No per-referral payments to anyone clinical** — physicians, hospital,
  SNF, home health, or hospice staff. Advisors get flat fees or equity;
  referral relationships are earned, not bought.
- Partner and advisor structures reviewed by a healthcare attorney before
  money moves.
- The Lou test on every placement. It costs a fee sometimes. It's why the
  fees keep coming.
