# Care Home Web Presence Screening — Outreach Targets

An initial web-presence screening of Triangle-area family care homes,
identifying which operators are hardest for families to find online — and
therefore the most receptive first calls for the placement service described
in [`SENIOR_CARE_PLACEMENT_STRATEGY.md`](SENIOR_CARE_PLACEMENT_STRATEGY.md)
and the outreach channels in
[`LEAD_GENERATION_STRATEGY.md`](LEAD_GENERATION_STRATEGY.md).

## Not completely invisible: Heart to Heart

Heart to Heart Family Care Home has a basic dedicated webpage with contact
information, so it should not be classified as having no presence. Its
presence is quite limited, however, and could still be substantially
improved — it belongs on the outreach list, just not in the "effectively
offline" tier.

## Strongest first calls, ranked

| # | Operator | Why it's a strong first call |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Pioneer Healthcare | Three homes under one operator — one relationship, three placement destinations |
| 2 | Divine Family Care Homes | Multiple homes in Franklinton and Youngsville |
| 3 | McCullers Home | Appears in generic directory listings but has no strong owned brand presence |
| 4 | House of Blessings at Sutton Road | Apparently unclaimed and minimally presented online |
| 5 | Grace Castle and Jolie's | Especially difficult for families to discover online at all |

## The two-part pitch

These operators are attractive because the approach carries two separate
offers at once:

> "I can send appropriately matched residents, and I can help families
> actually find and understand your home online."

The placement offer opens the door; the web-presence offer differentiates
the agency from every other referral source calling the same homes, and it
costs the operator nothing to hear out.

## Limits of this screening

This is an initial web screening, not a definitive audit. It did not
systematically check Google Maps, Facebook, Nextdoor, or private social
profiles. Before labeling any home "offline" in outreach materials, a full
audit should check each exact address and phone number across those
platforms.
