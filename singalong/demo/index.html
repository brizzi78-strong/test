How a session runs

Every circle follows the same 30–40 minute arc. In memory care the routine is the program — residents who can't hold "Tuesday at 2" can still feel "this is the singing time" when the gathering song starts.

  1. GatherHail, Hail, the Gang's All Here — the same opener every session, sung while everyone settles.
  2. GreetAround the circle, each resident greeted by name.
  3. Core songsFour familiar songs on the week's theme, lyrics on the shared screen. Slower and lower than the recordings — leave room for residents' voices.
  4. MoveOne up-tempo number with a simple motion: clap, sway, wave when she comes 'round the mountain.
  5. CloseGood Night, Ladies, softer, as a goodbye ritual. End calm, not on a peak.

The rules that keep it safe (and free)

Every song was published before 1931 or is traditional, so the music and these lyrics are public domain — nothing to license. Recordings stay whatever the facility already owns. Resident profiles are musical biography only: eras, genres, favorite artists, stories. Never diagnoses, medications, or care-plan data.

Residents

Musical background only — the intake questions that matter: What was playing when they were 15–25? What was sung at home, at church, at dances? What was their first-dance song?
