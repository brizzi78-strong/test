## 15.6 Note on evidence quality

Sections 15.4 and 15.5 comprise roughly one third of all citations. Where a claim is load-bearing
for the argument — Circle's distribution costs, Coinbase's revenue mix, Tether's reserve
composition, the Bybit incident timeline, the GENIUS Act vote counts — a source from §15.2 or §15.3
is cited. Where only press reporting exists, which is the case for most private-company valuations,
figures are treated as directional throughout and reported as ranges where outlets disagree
(§4.4, §13.5).
