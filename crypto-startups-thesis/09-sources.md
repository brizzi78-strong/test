## A note on source quality

Roughly a third of these are trade press rather than primary documents. Where a claim is
load-bearing for the argument — Circle's distribution costs, Coinbase's revenue mix, Tether's
reserve composition, the Bybit timeline, the GENIUS Act vote — a primary source (company press
release, SEC filing, or government notice) is cited. Where only trade press exists, which is the
case for most private-company valuations, the figure should be treated as directional.
