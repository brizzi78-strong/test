Process

  1. You send the genre, a short summary of the book and examples of covers you like.
  2. Within 7 days you get two cover concepts.
  3. Two rounds of revisions on the concept you choose.
  4. You get final files sized for your page count and printer.

Most covers take about three weeks.

Questions

Do I need my final page count to start?
No. It's only needed for the spine and back cover.

Do I own the cover?
Yes. You get full rights to the final design.

Can you match an existing series?
Yes. Send the earlier covers.

What about the ISBN barcode?
The back cover leaves space for it. KDP and IngramSpark add the barcode.
