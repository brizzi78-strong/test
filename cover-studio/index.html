FAQ

Common questions

Do I need my final page count to start?

No. It's only needed for the spine and back cover, so the front can start right away.

Do I own the cover?

Yes. You get full rights to the final design for every edition and all your marketing.

Can you match my series?

Yes. Send the earlier covers and the new one will fit right in.

What about the barcode?

The back cover leaves space for it. KDP and IngramSpark add the barcode for you.
