How it works

From brief to upload in about three weeks.

You'll see real covers early, at the size readers see them, not a mood board you have to imagine.

  1. Brief

    You send the genre, a short synopsis, three covers you love and any must-haves. A 30-minute call is optional.

  2. Concepts

    Within 7 days you get two distinct front covers, shown full size and as an Amazon thumbnail.

  3. Revisions

    Two rounds to refine type, color and imagery on the direction you pick.

  4. Files

    Final eBook and print files, sized to your page count and checked against the printer's template.

Questions

Before you book.

Do I need my final page count before we start?

Not for the front cover. The spine and back need a final page count, so we finish the print wrap once your interior is formatted.

Do I own the cover?

Yes. You receive full rights to the final design for your book, its editions and its marketing. Stock images carry their own license, which is bought in your name.

Can you match an existing series?

Yes. Send the earlier covers and the new title will follow the same type, layout and color system.

What about the ISBN and barcode?

Your ISBN goes on the copyright page and the back cover. KDP places a free barcode in a blank zone on the back cover, and IngramSpark supplies one with its template. Your cover leaves room for either.
