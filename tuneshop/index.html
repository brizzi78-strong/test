⚠️ Know before you tune
  • Warranty: performance software can void portions of your factory powertrain warranty. Magnuson-Moss protects you from blanket denials, but tune-related failures are on you.
  • Emissions: tunes marked off-road alter or require emissions-relevant hardware and are for closed-course use only. Not legal for street use in CARB states (California and others) — and increasingly enforced elsewhere.
  • Fuel: quoted gains assume 93 octane (91 for some maps). Running lower octane on a performance map can cause knock and engine damage.
  • Health check first: every tune starts with a full diagnostic scan and logs. We don't flash cars with pre-existing faults — fixing those comes first.
