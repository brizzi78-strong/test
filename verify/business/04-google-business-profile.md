# Google Business Profile — Copy & Setup

**Blue Ridge Press LLC** · set up free at business.google.com

*A Google Business Profile is one of the highest-ROI trust signals for a local service —
it puts you in Maps + local search and lets clients see who you are. Fill it out fully.*

---

## Core fields
- **Business name:** Blue Ridge Press LLC
- **Primary category:** *Background check service* (if unavailable, use *Employment agency*
  or *Screening service*; pick the closest Google offers)
- **Additional categories:** *Consultant*, *Employment agency*
- **Service area:** Raleigh & the Triangle, NC — and "serves customers online / nationwide"
  (set as a service-area business; you don't need a storefront address shown)
- **Phone / website:** [your business number] · https://blueridgepressllc.com
- **Hours:** By appointment (or your real hours)

## Short description (750 char max)
> Blue Ridge Press LLC runs consent-first background checks for people who need to trust
> their next hire — without breaking the bank. We verify professional references, past
> employment, and education straight from the source, only after the candidate e-signs
> their authorization. No snooping, no purchased data, no black-box scores — just honest
> confirmations you can stand behind. Ideal for families hiring in-home caregivers, small
> businesses making their first hires, landlords, and nonprofits vetting volunteers. For
> regulated criminal or credit records, we point you to a credentialed agency instead of
> faking it. Straightforward pricing from $39 per check. Serving Raleigh and clients
> nationwide online.

## Do
- Add 5–10 photos (logo, the oxblood brand, a clean workspace shot).
