# Record Retention Policy
**Blue Ridge Press LLC ("the Company") — Raleigh, North Carolina**

*This document is a starting template for the Company's internal use only. It is NOT legal advice. It must be reviewed, completed, and localized by a licensed attorney before the Company relies on it. It does not create, and must not imply, that any attorney or compliance professional prepared or reviewed it.*

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy states what records the Company keeps, how long it keeps them, how they are stored, and how they are securely destroyed. Retention is a balance: the Company keeps records long enough to support its work, meet its legal obligations, and defend itself if challenged — but no longer than necessary, to limit the amount of personal information it holds.

## 2. Scope

This policy covers all records the Company creates or receives while providing verification services, in any format (electronic or paper), including personal information about the individuals it verifies.

## 5. Legal Holds

If the Company reasonably anticipates litigation, an investigation, a regulatory inquiry, or a subpoena, it **suspends destruction** of all potentially relevant records immediately, regardless of the schedule above, until the owner/operator (with counsel) lifts the hold in writing. A legal hold always overrides the routine retention schedule.

## 6. Secure Storage

While retained, records are protected per the Information Security Policy:

- **Electronic records** are stored in access-controlled systems, encrypted at rest, and restricted to authorized personnel on a least-privilege basis.
- **Paper records**, if any, are kept in a locked location with restricted access.
- **No personal information** is stored in personal email, personal cloud accounts, or consumer chat apps.
- **Backups** are secured to the same standard as primary storage, and their retention is tracked so that deleted records do not persist indefinitely in backups. [Confirm backup retention with counsel.]

## 7. Access During Retention

Access to retained personal information is limited to personnel with a current business need. Access is logged where the system supports it. Sharing outside the Company follows the consent on file and applicable law.

## 8. Destruction / Deletion Procedure

When a record reaches the end of its retention period and is not under legal hold, the Company destroys it securely:

1. **Confirm eligibility.** Verify the retention period has elapsed and no legal hold applies.
2. **Electronic destruction.** Permanently delete files and purge from active systems; ensure removal from backups on the backup cycle; use secure-wipe methods for decommissioned devices/media.
3. **Paper destruction.** Cross-cut shred.
4. **Vendor-held data.** Direct any processor/vendor holding Company data to delete it on the same schedule, and obtain confirmation. [Track in vendor records.]
5. **Log the destruction.** Record what category was destroyed, the method, the date, and who performed it — without recording the personal content itself.

A **Destruction Log** is maintained and is itself retained for **[retention period]**.
