> **Important — FCRA Note:** This form uses concepts drawn from the federal Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA). Adopting it may cause Blue Ridge Press LLC to be treated as a **Consumer Reporting Agency** under the FCRA, which carries real obligations — including obligations to follow reasonable procedures to assure maximum possible accuracy and to reasonably confirm identity. Those obligations must be confirmed and localized by qualified counsel before this form is used.

---

## Purpose

This form helps Blue Ridge Press LLC ("the Company") reasonably confirm that the individual whose references, employment, and education will be verified is who they say they are. Confirming identity reduces the risk of verifying the wrong person or acting on a fraudulent request.

**Data minimization.** The Company collects only what is reasonably necessary to confirm identity and complete the requested verification. The Company does **not** store more sensitive identifiers than necessary, does **not** collect full Social Security numbers, dates of birth, or images of government IDs unless a specific, counsel-approved method requires it, and does **not** pull government records.
