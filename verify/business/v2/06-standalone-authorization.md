> **Important — FCRA Note (for Company/Client planning; not part of the authorization text below):** This document uses concepts drawn from the federal Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA). Providing verification results based on it may cause Blue Ridge Press LLC to be treated as a **Consumer Reporting Agency** under the FCRA. Under the FCRA the authorization must be **clear and conspicuous**, and it is typically paired with — but kept separate from — a standalone disclosure (see form 05). This authorization is designed to stand on its own page. Counsel must confirm and localize these requirements before use.

I have received and read the **Disclosure Regarding Verification of References, Employment, and Education** (form 05).

I authorize **Blue Ridge Press LLC**, and any company acting on its behalf, to contact the references and sources I provide and to confirm my **professional references, past employment, and education** for the purpose described in that disclosure.

I understand that:

- Blue Ridge Press LLC does **not** obtain criminal history, credit reports, driving records, or other government records.

I confirm that the information I provide about myself and my sources is accurate, and that I am authorizing this verification voluntarily.
