# Employment Verification Script / Call Guide

**Blue Ridge Press LLC — Professional Verification & Reference Services**

*This document is a starting template for the Company's internal use. It is NOT legal advice. The consent language and any legal or confidentiality items must be reviewed by a licensed North Carolina attorney before use.*

---

## How to use this script

This is a standardized guide for verifying a subject's **past employment** directly with the employer or its HR/verification office, **with the subject's consent**. The goal is to confirm objective facts the employer is willing to state — typically employer name, title, and dates. Many employers limit what they disclose; record only what they confirm. Do not press for opinions or private details.

**Before the call, confirm on file:**
- [ ] Subject's signed/e-signed consent authorizing employment verification
- [ ] Claimed employer, title, and dates as provided by the subject
- [ ] Tracking number: **[TRACKING #]**

---

## Part 1 — Introduction and Consent Confirmation

> "Hello, my name is **[Interviewer name]** with **Blue Ridge Press LLC**, an independent verification service. I'm confirming prior employment for **[Subject name]**, who has authorized this verification. Are you the right person to confirm employment details, or is there a verification line or process I should use?"

> "I only need to confirm a few factual employment details on record. We can provide written authorization from **[Subject name]** if your process requires it."

- [ ] Reached the correct person or verification channel
- [ ] Provided/authorization referenced if requested

---

## Part 2 — Facts to Confirm

1. Can you confirm that **[Subject name]** was employed at **[Employer name]**?
2. What was their job title (or titles)? *(confirm against claimed title: **[claimed title]**)*
3. What were their dates of employment — start and end? *(confirm against claimed dates: **[claimed dates]**)*
4. Was their employment full-time, part-time, or contract, if you're able to state that?
5. **Eligibility for rehire — only where the employer will state it:** Are you able to say whether **[Subject name]** is eligible for rehire? *(If the employer declines, record "employer does not disclose" — do not press.)*

> Note: Ask only for facts the employer is willing and able to confirm. Do not request reasons for leaving, performance opinions, salary (unless the client engagement and law permit and the employer volunteers it), or any private detail.

---

## Part 3 — Closing

> "Thank you. To confirm, you've verified **[title]** at **[Employer name]** for the period **[dates]**. That's all I need. I appreciate your help."

- [ ] Record what was confirmed vs. not disclosed
- [ ] Note source name/office and call date/time, interviewer initials
- [ ] Mark status: Confirmed / Consider / Unable to reach
- [ ] File against tracking number **[TRACKING #]**

---

## "Do Not Ask" Note

Confirm **objective employment facts only**. **Do not ask about, and do not record, any of the following** — even if the source raises it:

- Age, date of birth, or graduation year as a proxy for age
- Race, color, or national origin; citizenship or immigration status
- Religion or religious practices
- Sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, pregnancy, or family/marital status
- Disability, health, medical history, workers' compensation, or leave/absence for medical reasons
- Genetic information
- Any other legally protected characteristic

Also do not attempt to obtain **criminal history, credit history, or driving records** — those require a credentialed consumer-reporting agency and are outside the Company's scope. If a source volunteers protected-characteristic or out-of-scope information, do not record it; note only that it was unsolicited and not used.
