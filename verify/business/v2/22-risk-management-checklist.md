# Risk Management Checklist
**Blue Ridge Press LLC ("the Company") — Raleigh, North Carolina**

*This document is a starting template for the Company's internal use only. It is NOT legal advice. It must be reviewed, completed, and localized by a licensed attorney before the Company relies on it. It does not create, and must not imply, that any attorney or compliance professional prepared or reviewed it.*

---

## 1. Purpose

This is a periodic checklist the owner/operator works through to catch risks before they become problems. It is meant to be run on a set cadence and signed off. It does not replace legal review; several items below explicitly route to counsel.

## 2. How to Use

- Run this checklist **[quarterly / semi-annually — set cadence]** and after any significant change (new service, new vendor, new law).
- For each item, mark **OK / Needs attention / N/A**, add a note, and assign a follow-up owner and date for anything not OK.
- Record who completed the review and the date. Retain completed checklists per the Record Retention Policy.

## 3. Legal & FCRA Status

- [ ] Company's scope is still references / employment / education only — no criminal, credit, or MVR creep.
- [ ] Whether the Company is a Consumer Reporting Agency under the FCRA (and NC law) has been assessed by counsel, and the assessment is current. [Route to counsel.]
- [ ] Any dispute/reinvestigation obligations and deadlines are confirmed and being met (see Dispute Resolution Policy).
- [ ] Permissible-purpose documentation is required and collected for every engagement. [Counsel confirms standard.]
- [ ] Marketing and website claims match what the Company actually does and holds (no implied accreditation, certification, or insurance the Company lacks).

## 4. Consent on File

- [ ] Every active/completed file has valid, signed subject authorization covering the checks performed.
- [ ] Consent language is current and counsel-reviewed. [Route to counsel.]
- [ ] No verification proceeded without consent on file (spot-check a sample).
- [ ] Consent scope matches the scope of work actually performed.

## 5. Accuracy & Quality Assurance

- [ ] The Accuracy & QA SOP is being followed; reviewer sign-off is present on released reports.
- [ ] Two-source / confirmation rule applied to material facts; unverified items labeled honestly.
- [ ] Sample of recent reports re-checked for source traceability.
- [ ] Recurring error patterns identified and addressed.

## 6. Data Security

- [ ] MFA enabled on all accounts that reach personal information.
- [ ] Encryption at rest and in transit in place; device encryption verified.
- [ ] Access is least-privilege; departed people/vendors have had access revoked.
- [ ] No personal information found in personal email, personal cloud, or chat apps (spot-check).
- [ ] Backups exist, are secured, and have been test-restored.
- [ ] Access/logs reviewed for anomalies.

## 7. Vendor Risk

- [ ] Vendor inventory is current (who holds/processes Company data).
- [ ] Each vendor with data access has written data-protection terms. [Counsel confirms terms.]
- [ ] Vendor security posture (MFA, encryption, logging) reviewed.
- [ ] Terminated vendors have returned or deleted Company data, with confirmation.

## 8. Insurance

- [ ] Cyber-liability insurance status reviewed. **[Cyber-liability coverage, if/when obtained — record policy and limits here.]**
- [ ] Errors & Omissions / professional liability status reviewed. **[E&O coverage, if/when obtained.]**
- [ ] The Company is not representing coverage it does not hold.

## 9. Certifications & Memberships

- [ ] Certification/accreditation status reviewed. **[PBSA membership, SOC 2, ISO 27001, if/when obtained.]**
- [ ] No claim of certification or accreditation the Company does not currently hold appears in any Company material.

## 10. Complaint / Dispute Log

- [ ] Dispute Log is current; every dispute logged with reference number and dates.
- [ ] Reinvestigations completed within the required timeframe (see Dispute Resolution Policy). [Deadline confirmed by counsel.]
- [ ] Corrections issued and affected parties notified where required.
- [ ] Trends in complaints reviewed for underlying process issues.

## 11. Retention Compliance

- [ ] Records are being retained and destroyed on schedule (see Record Retention Policy).
- [ ] Destruction Log is current.
- [ ] Any legal holds are documented and active where applicable.
- [ ] No personal information retained past its period without a hold or documented reason.

## 12. Incident Preparedness

- [ ] Incident Response Plan contacts (counsel, technical support) are current.
- [ ] Any incidents since last review were handled and post-incident actions closed.
- [ ] Placeholders in the security and incident documents updated as facts become known.
