# Employee / Contractor Confidentiality & Data-Handling Agreement

**Blue Ridge Press LLC — Professional Verification & Reference Services**

*This document is a starting template for the Company's internal use. It is NOT legal advice. The legal, confidentiality, and data-handling provisions below must be reviewed and adapted by a licensed North Carolina attorney before the Company relies on or executes this agreement.*

---

## 1. Parties and Purpose

This Confidentiality & Data-Handling Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into between **Blue Ridge Press LLC**, a North Carolina limited liability company ("the Company"), and the individual identified in the signature block below ("you" or the "Signatory"), who performs services as an **[employee / independent contractor]** of the Company.

The Company provides independent, consent-based verification of professional **references, past employment, and education**. In performing services, you will be given access to sensitive personal information about verification subjects, clients, and the Company's own operations. This Agreement sets out your duties to keep that information confidential and to handle it securely.

---

## 2. Definitions

- **"Confidential Information"** means any non-public information you receive, access, or create in connection with your work for the Company, including but not limited to: verification subjects' personal data; consent records; client identities and requests; source contact details and responses; report contents and tracking numbers; the Company's methods, templates, pricing, and business records; and any information a reasonable person would understand to be confidential.
- **"Subject Data"** means personal information about an individual who is the subject of a verification (for example, a job candidate whose references, employment, or education are being verified with consent).
- **"Client Data"** means information about or provided by a client who has requested a verification.
- **"Personal Data"** means Subject Data and Client Data together, and any other information that identifies or can be linked to an identifiable individual.

---

## 3. Confidentiality Obligations

You agree that you will:

1. Hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and not disclose it to any person outside the Company except as required to perform your assigned work and as permitted by the Company.
2. Not use Confidential Information for any purpose other than performing services for the Company.
3. Not copy, transmit, remove, or store Confidential Information except on Company-approved systems and as necessary for your work.
4. Not discuss or disclose the existence, subject, or content of any verification with anyone other than authorized Company personnel and the specific source being contacted for that verification.
5. Immediately report to the Company any suspected or actual loss, unauthorized access, or disclosure of Confidential Information.

These obligations apply regardless of the form of the information (written, electronic, verbal, or observed).

---

## 4. Permitted Use and Scope

The Company's services are limited to verifying professional **references, past employment, and education**, in each case **with the documented consent of the subject** and confirmed **at the source**.

You agree that in the course of your work you will **not**:

- Attempt to obtain, verify, or report **criminal history, credit history, or motor vehicle records**. These are consumer reports that require a credentialed consumer-reporting agency; the Company does not provide them.
- Collect or record information about a subject's **protected characteristics** (including age, race, color, religion, sex, disability, marital or family status, pregnancy, national origin, or genetic information), or solicit such information from any source.
- Gather any personal information beyond what is necessary to complete the specific, consented verification you are assigned.

If a source volunteers information outside the permitted scope, do not record it in the report; note only that the information was not solicited and was not used.

---

## 5. Data-Security Duties

You agree to protect Personal Data and other Confidential Information by:

1. **Access:** Accessing only the records you need for an assigned task ("least-privilege" access).
2. **Devices:** Using only Company-approved devices and applications, kept current with security updates and protected by strong, unique passwords and, where available, multi-factor authentication.
3. **Storage:** Storing Personal Data only in Company-approved, access-controlled systems. Not storing Personal Data on personal drives, personal email, unmanaged cloud accounts, or removable media.
4. **Transmission:** Sending Personal Data only through Company-approved, encrypted channels. Not sending Personal Data over unsecured email, messaging, or public networks.
5. **Workspace:** Keeping screens, calls, and documents private; not discussing verifications in public places; locking devices when unattended.
6. **No secondary copies:** Not creating personal notes, screenshots, backups, or printouts of Personal Data except as the Company directs, and treating any such material as Confidential Information subject to return or destruction.
7. **Incident response:** Reporting any suspected security incident to the Company without delay and cooperating fully with the Company's response.

---

## 6. Return and Destruction of Data

Upon the end of your engagement, or at the Company's request at any time, you will promptly:

1. Return to the Company all materials containing Confidential Information in your possession or control; and
2. Permanently delete or destroy any remaining copies, in a manner approved by the Company, so that they cannot be recovered; and
3. If requested, provide the Company a written confirmation that you have complied with this Section.

You will not retain any Confidential Information after your engagement ends.

---

## 7. Term and Survival

This Agreement takes effect on the date you sign it and continues for as long as you have access to Confidential Information. Your confidentiality and data-handling obligations **survive** the end of your engagement and continue for as long as the information remains confidential or as required by applicable law, whichever is longer. *[Confirm any specific survival period with counsel.]*

All Confidential Information, work product, templates, and records remain the property of the Company or its clients. Nothing in this Agreement grants you any ownership or license to use them beyond performing your assigned work.
