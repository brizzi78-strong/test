## Disclosure

______________________________ (the "Company" or a company acting on its behalf, **Blue Ridge Press LLC**) may obtain a verification about you for a decision related to employment, tenancy, volunteering, or care placement.

The verification is limited to confirming:

- **Professional references** you provide;
- **Past employment** (such as dates, title, and eligibility for rehire, as disclosed by the source); and
- **Education** (such as attendance, program, and credential, as confirmed by the institution).

The Company will contact only the references and sources that you provide or authorize. The Company does **not** obtain criminal history, credit reports, driving records, or other government records.

You may ask for more information about the nature and scope of the verification.

*This is a disclosure only. It contains no waiver or release of any kind.*
