# Information Security Policy
**Blue Ridge Press LLC ("the Company") — Raleigh, North Carolina**

*This document is a starting template for the Company's internal use only. It is NOT legal advice. It must be reviewed, completed, and localized by a licensed attorney before the Company relies on it. It does not create, and must not imply, that any attorney or compliance professional prepared or reviewed it.*

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets the safeguards the Company uses to protect the personal information it handles when verifying references, employment, and education. It covers administrative, technical, and physical controls. The standard is simple: collect the minimum needed, protect it well, share it only with authorization, and dispose of it securely.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to the owner/operator and any employee, contractor, or vendor who accesses Company systems or data, and to all devices and accounts used for Company work.

## 3. Administrative Safeguards

- **Ownership.** The owner/operator (Rob Brizzi) is accountable for information security.
- **Least privilege.** Each person is granted only the access their work requires, and no more.
- **Onboarding/offboarding.** Access is granted deliberately and **revoked promptly** when a person, contractor, or vendor relationship ends.
- **Training.** Anyone handling personal information is briefed on this policy and on the Incident Response Plan before they begin.
- **Written procedures.** Security procedures are documented and followed, not improvised.

## 4. Access Control

- Unique accounts per person — **no shared logins**.
- Access to personal information is limited to a current business need and reviewed periodically.
- Administrative/privileged access is limited to the owner/operator and granted to others only when strictly necessary.
- Access is logged where systems support it.

## 5. Authentication

- **Multi-factor authentication (MFA)** is required on all accounts that hold or can reach personal information, wherever the service supports it — email, storage, and any verification tooling.
- Strong, unique passwords are required; **reuse across services is prohibited**.
- Passwords are stored in a reputable password manager, never in plaintext files, spreadsheets, notes, or browsers on shared devices.
- Credentials are never shared by email or chat.

## 6. Encryption

- **In transit:** all transmission of personal information uses encrypted channels (HTTPS/TLS, encrypted email or secure file transfer). Personal information is not sent over unencrypted channels.
- **At rest:** personal information is stored on encrypted storage; device full-disk encryption is enabled on every device used for Company work.

## 7. Device Security

- Devices used for Company work have full-disk encryption, an automatic screen lock, a strong device passcode, and current operating-system and application updates.
- Reputable endpoint protection/anti-malware is enabled.
- Devices are not left unattended and unlocked in public or shared spaces.
- Lost or stolen devices are treated as a potential incident under the Incident Response Plan and are remotely wiped where possible.

## 8. Handling Personal Information — Do / Don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Keep personal information in approved, access-controlled Company systems | Store or forward PII in **personal email, personal cloud, or consumer chat apps** |
| Share PII only with authorized parties, per consent on file | Text, DM, or paste PII into unapproved tools |
| Collect only what is needed for the verification | Keep PII "just in case" beyond retention |
| Redact/minimize PII in working documents where possible | Print PII unnecessarily |

## 9. Vendor / Third-Party Management

- Before a vendor touches personal information, assess its security and require, in writing, that it protect the data to at least this policy's standard and use it only for the Company's purposes. [Confirm contract terms with counsel.]
- Prefer vendors that support MFA, encryption, and access logging.
- Keep an inventory of vendors that hold or process Company data and review it periodically.
- On termination, require the vendor to return or securely delete Company data and confirm deletion.

## 10. Physical Safeguards

- Any paper containing personal information is kept in a locked location with restricted access and is never left in the open.
- Work areas are kept clear of exposed personal information (clean-desk practice).
- Visitors do not have access to areas or screens where personal information is displayed.

## 11. Secure Disposal

- Electronic media is securely wiped or destroyed before disposal, resale, or reassignment.
- Paper containing personal information is cross-cut shredded.
- Disposal follows the schedule and logging in the Record Retention Policy.

## 12. Monitoring and Incidents

- Review access and available logs periodically for anomalies.
- Any suspected compromise is reported immediately and handled under the Incident Response Plan.

## 13. Insurance and Certifications

The Company does not currently hold cyber-liability or errors-and-omissions insurance, and does not currently hold any third-party security certification. **[Cyber-liability / E&O insurance, if/when obtained]** and **[SOC 2 / ISO 27001 / PBSA or other certification, if/when obtained]** would be recorded here. The Company does not represent that it holds any coverage or certification it has not actually obtained.

## 14. Review

This policy is reviewed at least annually and after any material change in systems, vendors, or applicable law.
