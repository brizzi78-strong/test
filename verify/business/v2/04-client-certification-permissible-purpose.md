## Purpose of This Certification

Before Blue Ridge Press LLC ("the Company") performs any verification of professional references, past employment, or education, the business client requesting the verification ("the Client") must complete and sign this certification. The Company relies on the accuracy of the Client's certifications below.

The Company verifies **references, past employment, and education only**. The Company does **not** provide criminal history, credit, driving/MVR, or other regulated background-screening products.

1. **Permissible purpose.** The Client is requesting this verification for a permissible business purpose, which is (check all that apply):
   - ☐ Employment (hiring, retention, promotion, or reassignment)
   - ☐ Tenancy / housing decision
   - ☐ Volunteer screening
   - ☐ Care or caregiving placement decision
   - ☐ Other lawful purpose: ______________________________

2. **Consent obtained.** Before the Client submits any individual's information, the Client has provided the individual with a clear, standalone disclosure and has obtained the individual's signed written authorization to conduct this verification. The Client will retain a copy and provide it to the Company on request.

3. **No prohibited use.** The Client will not use the verification, in whole or in part, for any purpose prohibited by law, and will not use it to discriminate against any individual in violation of any applicable federal, state, or local law.

4. **Accuracy of request.** The information the Client provides about the individual and the sources to be contacted is, to the Client's knowledge, accurate.

5. **Adverse action.** If the Client takes any adverse action (for example, declining to hire, rent to, or place the individual) based in whole or in part on the verification results, the Client will follow all adverse-action steps required by applicable law. Where the FCRA applies, this may include providing the individual, before taking final adverse action, a copy of the report and a summary of their rights, and providing a notice after the adverse action. Counsel must confirm the exact steps and timing.

6. **Lawful handling.** The Client will keep the verification results confidential, use them only for the certified purpose, and dispose of them securely when no longer needed.
