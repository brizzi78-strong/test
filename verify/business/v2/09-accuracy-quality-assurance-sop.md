# Accuracy & Quality Assurance SOP
**Blue Ridge Press LLC ("the Company") — Raleigh, North Carolina**

*This document is a starting template for the Company's internal use only. It is NOT legal advice. It must be reviewed, completed, and localized by a licensed attorney before the Company relies on it. It does not create, and must not imply, that any attorney or compliance professional prepared or reviewed it.*

---

## 1. Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) sets the standards every verification report must meet before it is released. The goal is simple: every fact in a report is traceable to a legitimate source, verified to a defined standard, and reviewed by a second person before the report leaves the Company.

The Company verifies professional references, past employment, and education only, and only with the Individual's documented consent.

## 2. Principles

1. **Traceability.** Every stated fact maps to a documented source and date.
2. **Consent first.** No verification begins without valid, current authorization on file.
3. **Accuracy over completeness.** An unverified fact is labeled unverified; it is never presented as confirmed.
4. **Neutrality.** The Company records what sources actually say, without embellishment or interpretation.
5. **Second-set-of-eyes.** No report is released on a single person's judgment alone.

## 3. Before Verification Begins

The preparer confirms and records:

- [ ] Signed consent/authorization from the Individual is on file and covers the specific checks requested.
- [ ] The scope requested is within what the Company offers (references, employment, education only).
- [ ] The Individual's identifying details are sufficient to match records to the correct person.
- [ ] The Client's request and permissible purpose are documented. [Counsel to confirm permissible-purpose standard.]

If any item is missing, verification does not start.

## 4. Source-Verification Standards

Sources are ranked by reliability. The preparer always seeks the most authoritative source reasonably available.

| Check | Preferred (primary) source | Acceptable secondary source |
|---|---|---|
| Past employment | Employer HR / official records | Documented direct supervisor; official pay/tenure record |
| Education | Registrar / institution / official verification service | Official transcript provided and confirmed with issuer |
| Professional reference | The named reference, contacted directly and identity confirmed | Documented alternate contact confirmed as authorized |

Standards for every source contact:

1. **Confirm the source's identity and authority** before recording any information (right person, right role, right institution).
2. **Contact the source directly** through an independently confirmed channel — not a phone number or email supplied only by the subject without corroboration.
3. **Record verbatim or closely paraphrased responses**, the date, the method, and who was spoken with.
4. **Do not lead the source.** Ask neutral, open questions.

## 5. Two-Source / Confirmation Rule

- A **material fact** (employment dates, title, degree, dates of attendance, whether a reference endorses the Individual) is confirmed by the primary authoritative source.
- Where the primary source is unavailable, unresponsive, or ambiguous, the fact must be **corroborated by a second independent source** before it is reported as confirmed.
- If only a single, non-authoritative source supports a material fact, the report marks it **"reported by [source], not independently confirmed."**
- Conflicting information from two sources is reported as a **discrepancy**, showing both, never silently resolved.

## 6. Handling Unclear, Partial, or Declined Responses

| Situation | Required handling |
|---|---|
| Source declines to confirm | Record "source declined to confirm"; do not infer the underlying fact |
| Source unreachable after reasonable attempts | Record number and dates of attempts; mark item "unable to verify" |
| Response is ambiguous | Seek clarification; if unresolved, report as ambiguous, quoting the response |
| "Neutral policy" employer (dates/title only) | Report only what was confirmed; note the policy limitation |
| Suspected fraudulent document/source | Halt; escalate to the owner/operator; do not report the item as confirmed |

The Company defines "reasonable attempts" as **[X] contact attempts across at least [X] business days by at least [two] channels** where available. [Confirm/adjust.]

## 7. Reviewer Checklist (Before Sign-Off)

The reviewer is a different person from the preparer (or, where the Company is a single operator, a documented self-review pass performed against this checklist on a separate day, until a second reviewer is available). The reviewer confirms:

- [ ] Valid consent covers every check performed.
- [ ] Every fact in the report links to a documented source and date in the working file.
- [ ] Material facts meet the two-source / confirmation rule, or are clearly labeled as unconfirmed.
- [ ] No criminal, credit, or MVR information is present (out of scope).
- [ ] Unverified, declined, and ambiguous items are labeled honestly.
- [ ] Discrepancies are shown, not resolved by assumption.
- [ ] No opinion or inference is presented as verified fact.
- [ ] Individual identity is correctly matched throughout.
- [ ] Spelling of names, employers, institutions, dates, and titles is correct.
- [ ] The report scope matches what the Client requested and what consent allows.

## 8. Sign-Off and Release

1. The reviewer records the review date and their name/initials in the working file.
2. Any item the reviewer flags is returned to the preparer and re-verified before release.
3. The report is released only after all flagged items are resolved and the checklist is complete.
4. The final report and the completed checklist are saved to the working file per the Record Retention Policy.

## 9. Corrections After Release

If an error is found after release, the Company follows the Dispute Resolution Policy, issues a corrected report, and notifies affected parties as that policy requires.

## 10. Review

This SOP is reviewed at least annually and updated when sources, methods, or applicable law change.
