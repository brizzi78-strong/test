## 1. Overview

1.1 Blue Ridge Press LLC ("the Company," "we," "us," or "our") provides independent, consent-based verification of professional references, past employment, and education. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why, how we use and share it, and the rights available to individuals.

1.2 We interact with two main groups: **Clients** (businesses that request verifications) and **Subjects** (individuals whose references, employment, or education are being verified with their consent). This Policy applies to both, as well as to visitors to our Site.

1.3 We do not collect or provide criminal, credit, or motor-vehicle records. See Section 3.

## 2. Information We Collect

| Category | Examples | Typical source |
|---|---|---|
| Contact and identity | Name, email, phone, employer, job title | Client, Subject |
| Verification input | References provided, past employers, dates, positions, schools, degrees, consent documentation | Subject, Client |
| Verification results | Confirmations, non-confirmations, and responses from references, employers, and institutions | Third-party verification sources |
| Account and billing | Account credentials, billing contact, transaction records | Client |
| Site/technical | IP address, device/browser data, cookies, usage logs | Automatically via the Site |

2.1 We ask that Clients and Subjects provide only the information necessary for the requested verification, and that no sensitive categories (such as government identifiers) be submitted unless specifically requested and lawful.

## 3. What We Do NOT Collect or Provide

3.1 The Company does **not** collect, compile, or furnish criminal history, credit information, or motor-vehicle/driving records. Those are regulated products that require a credentialed consumer-reporting agency, which the Company is not unless separately credentialed ("[Accreditation, if/when obtained]").

3.2 **FCRA note.** Concepts such as permissible purpose, disclosure, authorization, and dispute/reinvestigation come from the federal Fair Credit Reporting Act ("FCRA"). Adopting these practices may cause the Company to be treated as a "consumer reporting agency" under the FCRA, with significant legal obligations. Counsel must confirm the Company's status and obligations before any FCRA-regulated data handling is undertaken.

## 4. How We Use Information

4.1 We use personal information to:

- (a) perform the requested reference, employment, and education verifications;
- (b) confirm and document Subject consent;
- (c) communicate with Clients and Subjects about an engagement;
- (d) process payment and maintain records;
- (e) operate, secure, and improve the Site and Services; and
- (f) comply with legal obligations.

4.2 We do not use verification information for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making about Subjects.

## 5. Legal and Consent Basis

5.1 **Consent-first.** Our core processing of Subject information rests on the Subject's informed, documented consent to be verified, obtained by the Client before the engagement.

5.2 **GDPR lawful bases (where applicable).** If we process personal data of individuals in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we rely on one or more lawful bases under the GDPR/UK GDPR, which may include: consent (Art. 6(1)(a)); performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)); and legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)), balanced against the individual's rights. The applicable basis and any controller/processor roles must be confirmed by counsel. [Confirm whether the Company serves EEA/UK data subjects.]

5.3 **CCPA/CPRA (where applicable).** For California residents, this Policy, together with any notice-at-collection presented at or before the point of collection, describes the categories of personal information we collect and the purposes for which they are used. We do not use California residents' personal information for incompatible purposes without notice.

## 6. No Sale or Sharing for Advertising

6.1 **We do not sell personal information, and we do not "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising**, as those terms are used under the CCPA/CPRA. We do not trade personal information for money or other valuable consideration.

## 7. When and With Whom We Share

7.1 We disclose personal information only as needed to deliver the Services or as required by law, including:

- (a) **Verification sources** — the references, former employers, and educational institutions we contact to confirm information the Subject authorized;
- (b) **The requesting Client** — the results of the verification they engaged us to perform;
- (c) **Service providers/processors** — vendors that support hosting, security, and payment under written confidentiality and data-protection terms;
- (d) **Legal and safety** — where required by law, legal process, or to protect rights and safety.

7.2 Service providers are permitted to use personal information only to perform services for us and not for their own purposes.

10.1 **All individuals.** Subjects and other individuals may request to access, correct, or delete their personal information held by us, subject to legal limits and verification of identity. Because much of the information we hold originates with the Client or the Subject, some requests may need to be directed to, or coordinated with, the Client.

10.3 **EEA/UK residents (GDPR/UK GDPR).** Subject to conditions, you may have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, objection, and to withdraw consent, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.
