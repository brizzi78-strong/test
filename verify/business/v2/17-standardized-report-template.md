## Scope and Limitations

- This report covers **only** the consent-based verification of professional **references, past employment, and education** confirmed at the source.
- This report **does not** include and must **not** be relied upon for **criminal history, credit history, or motor vehicle records**. Those are consumer reports requiring a credentialed consumer-reporting agency, which Blue Ridge Press LLC is not.
- Findings reflect information provided by the sources contacted. The Company reports what sources confirm and does not independently guarantee the truthfulness of a source's statements.
- "Unable to reach" means a source did not respond within the outreach window; it is not a negative finding.
- No protected-characteristic information was solicited or used in preparing this report.
- This report is confidential and intended solely for the requesting client and its permitted use. *[Client use for employment decisions to be governed by the service agreement and confirmed with counsel.]*
