# Education / Credential Verification Script

**Blue Ridge Press LLC — Professional Verification & Reference Services**

*This document is a starting template for the Company's internal use. It is NOT legal advice. The consent language and any legal or confidentiality items must be reviewed by a licensed North Carolina attorney before use.*

---

## How to use this script

This is a standardized guide for verifying a subject's **education or credential** directly with the institution's registrar, records office, or authorized verification service (for example, a national clearinghouse), **with the subject's consent**. Confirm only the objective facts the institution will release. Many institutions require written authorization from the subject before disclosing records; be ready to provide it.

**Before the call, confirm on file:**
- [ ] Subject's signed/e-signed consent/records-release authorizing education verification
- [ ] Claimed institution, program/degree, and dates as provided by the subject
- [ ] Tracking number: **[TRACKING #]**

---

## Part 1 — Introduction and Consent Confirmation

> "Hello, my name is **[Interviewer name]** with **Blue Ridge Press LLC**, an independent verification service. I'm confirming an educational credential for **[Subject name]**, who has authorized this verification and signed a records release. Is this the right office to confirm degree and attendance records, or should I use a specific verification process?"

- [ ] Reached registrar / records office / authorized verification service
- [ ] Records-release authorization provided if required

---

## Part 2 — Facts to Confirm

1. Can you confirm that **[Subject name]** attended **[Institution name]**? *(Include any prior name on record if the subject's name has changed.)*
2. What were the dates of attendance or enrollment? *(confirm against claimed dates: **[claimed dates]**)*
3. Was a degree, diploma, or credential awarded? If so, which one? *(confirm against claimed credential: **[claimed credential]**)*
4. What was the field of study or major, if the institution will state it?
5. What was the date the degree or credential was conferred (graduation date)?
6. If applicable: can you confirm the credential is currently valid / in good standing? *(for licenses or certifications, where the issuer will state it)*

> Note: Confirm only records the institution will release. Do not request grades, transcripts, disciplinary records, or financial information unless the engagement, the subject's authorization, and applicable law specifically permit it.

---

## Part 3 — Closing

> "Thank you. To confirm, you've verified **[credential/degree]** in **[field]** from **[Institution name]**, conferred **[date]**. That's everything I need. I appreciate your help."

- [ ] Record what was confirmed vs. not disclosed
- [ ] Note source office and call/response date/time, interviewer initials
- [ ] Mark status: Confirmed / Consider / Unable to reach
- [ ] File against tracking number **[TRACKING #]**

---

## "Do Not Ask" Note

Confirm **objective education/credential facts only**. **Do not ask about, and do not record, any of the following** — even if the source raises it:

- Age or date of birth *(a graduation year may be confirmed as a record fact, but never used to infer or record age)*
- Race, color, or national origin; citizenship or immigration status
- Religion or religious practices (including a religious institution as a proxy for belief)
- Sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, pregnancy, or family/marital status
- Disability, health, or medical history; accommodation or leave records
- Genetic information
- Any other legally protected characteristic

Do not gather disciplinary, financial, or health records outside the consented scope, and do not attempt criminal, credit, or driving-record checks — those are outside the Company's scope. If a source volunteers protected-characteristic or out-of-scope information, do not record it; note only that it was unsolicited and not used.
