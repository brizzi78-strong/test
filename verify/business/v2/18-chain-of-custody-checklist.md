# Secure Chain-of-Custody Checklist

**Blue Ridge Press LLC — Professional Verification & Reference Services**

*This document is a starting template for the Company's internal use. It is NOT legal advice. The data-handling, consent, and integrity provisions must be reviewed by a licensed North Carolina attorney before use.*

---

## Purpose

This checklist tracks a single verification report from the client's request through delivery, creating a clear record of **who handled the data, when, and how it stayed secure and unaltered**. Complete one checklist per report and file it against the tracking number. Every step should be initialed and timestamped by the handler who performed it.
