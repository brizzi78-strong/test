## 1. Scope of Services

1.1 The Company will provide **independent, consent-based verification of professional references, past employment, and education** (the "Services"), as further described in one or more order forms or statements of work ("SOWs") that reference this Agreement.

1.2 **Exclusions.** The Services do **not** include criminal history, credit, or motor-vehicle/driving records, or any other product requiring a credentialed consumer-reporting agency. The Company is not a consumer reporting agency and does not provide such products unless separately and expressly credentialed ("[Accreditation, if/when obtained]").

1.3 The Services are informational. The Company reports verification results and does not make hiring, eligibility, or adverse-action decisions for Client.

## 2. Client Obligations

2.1 **Permissible purpose and lawful use.** Client will request and use the Services only for a lawful, legitimate business purpose and in compliance with all applicable laws.

2.2 **Consent.** Client represents and warrants that, before each verification request, it has obtained valid, current, written consent from the individual being verified (the "Subject"), and Client will provide evidence of that consent to the Company on request.

2.3 **Accurate input.** Client will provide accurate and complete information necessary to perform the Services and will not submit sensitive data beyond what the Services require.

2.4 **No prohibited use.** Client will not use the Services or results for any unlawful, discriminatory, or deceptive purpose, and will not use results as a substitute for a regulated consumer report (criminal, credit, or motor-vehicle screening).

2.5 **FCRA note.** Certain concepts — permissible purpose, disclosure, authorization, and dispute/reinvestigation — originate in the federal Fair Credit Reporting Act ("FCRA"). Client acknowledges that adopting or requiring these functions may cause the Company to be treated as a "consumer reporting agency" under the FCRA, with significant legal obligations. The Parties intend the Services to remain outside the FCRA framework, and each Party's counsel must confirm the Company's status and obligations before any FCRA-regulated activity is undertaken.
