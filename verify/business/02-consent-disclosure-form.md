# Background Check — Disclosure & Authorization

**Blue Ridge Press LLC**

> **INTERNAL NOTE (delete before sending to candidates):** This is a plain-language
> starting draft, **not legal advice**. Have a background-screening / FCRA attorney
> review and finalize it before use. If your attorney determines you are a Consumer
> Reporting Agency, FCRA requires the *disclosure* to be a **standalone document**
> (its own page, no other content, no liability waivers mixed in) and the
> *authorization* to be clear and conspicuous. Keep the signed copy with a timestamp.

---

## Part 1 — Disclosure

Blue Ridge Press LLC has been asked by **[Requesting Party / Employer]** to confirm
information you have provided, so they can make an informed decision about
**[the position / role / engagement]**.

With your permission, we will contact the sources **you provide** and confirm:

- **Professional references** — your relationship, dates, and whether they recommend you
- **Past employment** — role, dates, and (where the employer will say) rehire eligibility
- **Education / credentials** — attendance and the degree or credential earned

**What we do *not* do:** We do **not** obtain criminal history, credit reports, or
driving records; we are not a consumer-reporting agency for those. We do **not** search
social media or purchase third-party background files. We only confirm what you and your
own listed sources attest to.

Nothing is contacted or checked until you sign the authorization below.
