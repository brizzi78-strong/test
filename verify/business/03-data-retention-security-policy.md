# Data Retention & Security Policy

**Blue Ridge Press LLC — internal operating policy**

*Starting template, not legal advice. Align with your attorney's guidance and any
FCRA / state-law requirements that apply to your model.*

---

## 1. What we collect
- **Client (requesting party):** name, business, contact details, and the details of
  the person to be verified.
- **Candidate:** identifying details needed to run the check, the references / employers
  / schools they provide, and their signed authorization (with timestamp).
- **Sources:** the confirmations a reference, employer, or registrar chooses to give.

We do **not** collect criminal, credit, or driving records, SSNs, or purchased
background files.

## 2. Purpose limitation
Information is used **only** to perform the requested verification and deliver the report
to the requesting party. It is never sold, used for advertising, or used to build profiles.

## 3. Storage & access
- Stored in the Verify system (access-controlled) and, where used, our e-signature and
  email tools.
- **Access is limited to the owner/operator.** Anyone with access is bound by this policy.
- Devices used for the business are password/passphrase protected and, where possible,
  disk-encrypted.
- No candidate PII is kept in personal email, texts, spreadsheets, or chat tools.

## 4. Retention schedule
| Record | Kept for | Then |
|---|---|---|
| Signed consent / authorization | Duration of engagement + **[X] years** per counsel | Deleted |
| Completed report | Delivery + **[X] months** for client re-download | Deleted |
| Working notes / draft data | Until report delivered | Deleted |

*(Fill in the retention periods your attorney recommends; screening records are often
kept a defined number of years for dispute/audit reasons.)*

## 5. Deletion & candidate rights
- Candidates and clients may ask what is held about them, request corrections, or request
  deletion.
- On a valid deletion request (subject to any legal retention obligation), records are
  removed from the Verify system and connected tools.

## 6. Breach response
If PII is exposed or lost: (1) contain it, (2) assess what was affected, (3) notify
affected people and any parties required by law promptly, (4) fix the cause and record
what happened.

## 7. Vendors
Service providers (e-signature, email, hosting) are used only as needed to run checks and
are expected to maintain reasonable security. Review their terms before adding new ones.
