How it works

Six steps. Consent first. Proof at the end.

From your request to a report anyone can verify is genuine — here is exactly what happens, and what never does.

1
You · 2 minutes

Tell us who to verify

Send the candidate’s name and email, and who should confirm what — references, past employers, schools. Nothing is contacted yet.

2
The candidate

They consent — or nothing happens

The candidate receives a private link, reads a plain-language disclosure listing exactly who will be contacted and about what, and e-signs. The signature is recorded with a timestamp and the exact disclosure they saw. Until they sign, every verification link is locked — enforced by the system, not by promise.

3
The sources

Each source answers directly

Every reference, employer, and registrar gets their own private, one-time link — a two-minute form to confirm the relationship, dates, and whether they’d recommend or rehire. Straight from the people who were actually there. No data brokers, ever.

4
Quality review

We check it before you see it

Findings are reviewed for accuracy and completeness against our QA standard. Anything a source declined or contradicted is stated plainly — flagged for your awareness, never smoothed over.

5
Your report

One clean, sealed report

You receive an executive PDF: every finding, the consent record, a unique tracking number, and a cryptographic integrity seal computed from the exact findings — alter one word and the seal breaks.

6
Anyone, any time

The report proves itself

Every report carries a QR code and verification code. Scan it and our public verify page confirms the report is authentic and unaltered — showing only the candidate’s initials and the result, never their private details. Share it with a partner, a co-owner, a board: it holds up.

What we never do

  • No checks without consent. Not one call, not one email, before the candidate signs.
  • No criminal, credit, or driving records. Those are regulated consumer reports that belong with a credentialed agency — we’ll refer you to one instead of pretending.
  • No SSNs, no scraping, no purchased files, no black-box scores. We confirm what real people attest to, and nothing else.

Typical turnaround: a few business days — it depends on how quickly sources respond. You can see status at every step, and the report notes exactly who responded and when.
