About

Trust, verified — the honest way.

Why Blue Ridge Press LLC checks people the way a careful person would, if they had the time.

Blue Ridge Press LLC exists for one idea: you should be able to trust the people you let into your business and your home — without spying on anyone to do it.

Most background screening sits at two extremes. The big vendors sell expensive, database-driven reports padded with data you never asked for. The cheap ones scrape the internet and sell you strangers’ private lives. Neither actually tells you what you need to know: can this person be trusted, and will the people who know their work say so?

A different way to check someone

We do it the honest way — the way a careful person would do it themselves, if they had the time. We ask the candidate’s permission, in writing. Then we go straight to the source: former managers, past employers, the school that granted the credential. We confirm what’s real and we report it plainly.

No snooping. No purchased files. No mystery risk scores. Just verified facts and the word of people who were actually there.

Why it started with caregivers

This work grew out of helping families care for aging parents. When you hire someone to come into your mother’s home, a database check doesn’t tell you what a former family they cared for will. That’s the check that matters — and almost no one was offering it affordably. So we built it.

The same honest check that protects a family hiring a caregiver protects a small business making its first hire, a landlord choosing a tenant, or a nonprofit vetting a volunteer.

Our promise

  • Consent comes first. Every check runs on the candidate’s written, e-signed authorization — recorded, timestamped, kept.
  • We stay in our lane. We verify references, employment, and education. For regulated records, we refer you to a credentialed agency instead of pretending.
  • People over data. We confirm what a person and their references attest to, and we never dig through anyone’s private life.

Blue Ridge Press LLC is a small, owner-run company. When you reach out, you reach a person who will actually stand behind the report with their name on it.
