Our standard

Defined as much by what we won't do as what we will.

A premium verification firm earns trust through restraint. We confirm what can be confirmed honestly — and we tell you plainly where the line is.

What we do

  • Verify professional references directly with the people who worked with the candidate.
  • Confirm past employment — role, dates, and rehire eligibility where the employer will state it.
  • Confirm education and credentials with the institution.
  • Record written, time‑stamped consent for every check.

What we don't

  • Pull criminal, credit, or driving records — those are regulated and belong with a credentialed consumer‑reporting agency, which we'll refer you to.
  • Scrape social media or purchase third‑party data files.
  • Assign black‑box "risk scores" you can't see behind.
  • Contact anyone before the candidate has authorized it.

Principles

Four commitments behind every report.

Consent first

Every check runs only on the candidate's written, e‑signed authorization — recorded with a timestamp and the exact disclosure they agreed to.

Verified at the source

Real managers, real employers, real registrars. We confirm with the people who were actually there — never a data broker.

Documented

Each report carries a tracking number and a chain of custody — who confirmed what, and when — so it stands up to scrutiny.

Confidential

Reports go only to the requesting party, on a defined retention schedule, handled under a written security policy.

How an engagement runs

Five steps. Every one on the record.

01

Request

You tell us who to verify and which sources to confirm. Nothing is contacted yet.

02

Consent

The candidate reviews a plain‑language disclosure and e‑signs their authorization. It's recorded and time‑stamped — enforced, not promised.

03

Source verification

We reach each reference, employer, and institution directly and confirm the facts, following a standardized interview script.

04

Quality review

Findings are checked for accuracy and completeness against our QA standard before anything is released.

05

Delivered report

You receive one clean, documented report — tracking number, consent record, and every confirmation in one place.
