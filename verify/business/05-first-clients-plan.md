# First-Clients Plan — First 30 Days

**Blue Ridge Press LLC** · goal: land your first 3–5 paying checks and one referral partner.

*You don't need marketing spend. You need 20 real conversations. This is a plan for that.*

---

## Who to target first (tightest fit → widest)
1. **Families hiring in-home help for an aging parent** — your sharpest wedge and the
   reason the book exists. They're handing a stranger the keys to Mom's house.
2. **Small business owners making an early hire** — restaurants, salons, trades, clinics.
3. **Independent landlords** vetting a tenant or a handyman.
4. **Nonprofits / churches / youth & senior programs** vetting volunteers.

## The one-sentence pitch
> "I run honest, consent-based reference checks — I actually call a person's former
> managers and references and confirm they're who they say — for a fraction of what the
> big screening companies charge, starting at $39."

## Referral partners (the real leverage)
These people meet your buyers every day. One good partner > 100 cold posts.
- **Home-care & senior-care agencies** (they place caregivers; families still want
  independent vetting)
- **Elder-law attorneys & geriatric care managers**
- **Property-management companies & realtors**
- **Small-business bookkeepers / accountants** (they know every new employer)
- **Staffing / recruiting micro-agencies** without their own screening

**The ask:** "When you meet someone hiring a caregiver / a first employee / a tenant and
they're nervous about who they're trusting, send them my way. Their first check is on me."

## 30-day plan
**Week 1 — set the table**
- Finish site (favicon, social image, confirm pages render), business email, Google profile.
- Write your list: 20 people you already know in the 4 buyer groups + 10 potential partners.

**Week 2 — warm outreach**
- Message all 20 personally (not a blast): "I started an honest, affordable background-check
  service — mind if I tell you what it does in case you or someone you know is hiring?"
- Offer the **first check free** to 3 people whose feedback + testimonial you want.

**Week 3 — partners**
- Coffee / call with 5 potential referral partners. Bring the one-pager and the free-check
  offer for their referrals.
- Post the caregiver angle where your buyers already are (local parenting/caregiver groups,
  church bulletins, neighborhood boards) — helpful, not salesy.

**Week 4 — convert + prove**
- Run the free/first checks, deliver clean reports, **ask each happy client for**:
  (1) a Google review, (2) a one-line testimonial for the site, (3) one referral.
- Send your first real invoice / pay-link. Bank a paying client.

## The mindset
Your competitor isn't Checkr — it's the busy person who *means* to call references and
never does. Sell "someone actually did the diligence, with consent, for my one hire."
Do 5 of those flawlessly and the referrals start doing the selling for you.
