# Blue Ridge Press LLC — Background-Check Launch Checklist

*Working checklist to take the consent-based screening business from "live site" to
"official, client-ready." Not legal advice — the FCRA item below needs a real attorney.*

---

## ⚠️ FIRST — the item that gates everything
- [ ] **Consult a background-screening / FCRA attorney about CRA status.**
  Even consent-based reference / employment / education checks, when a third party
  uses them for a **hiring decision**, can legally be "consumer reports" — which can
  make you a **Consumer Reporting Agency** under the FCRA, even with no criminal or
  credit data. Get a clear answer: either "your model stays outside CRA territory,
  here's how to keep it there," or "you're a CRA, here's how to comply." **Do not run
  a paid check for a real employer until you have this answer.**

## Phase 1 — Legal foundation
- [ ] Resolve the entity: confirm with **NC Secretary of State** which LLC is
      registered and in good standing (QuickBooks says *Blue Ridge Financial Coaching
      LLC*; the business is *Blue Ridge Press LLC*), and which one the checks run under.
- [ ] Obtain / confirm **EIN** for that entity (IRS, free).
- [ ] File **DBA / assumed name** if operating under a name ≠ the registered LLC
      (Wake County Register of Deeds).
- [ ] Check **City of Raleigh / Wake County** local business-license requirements.
- [ ] **Attorney-reviewed** Privacy Policy + Terms (drafts already live on the site).
- [ ] **FCRA-compliant disclosure + authorization form** finalized by counsel
      (starting draft: `02-consent-disclosure-form.md`).
- [ ] **E&O / professional liability insurance** (screening-specific) — get quotes.
