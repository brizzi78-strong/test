### Tips
- Send these **one at a time, personally.** No mass blasts — people can smell it.
- Lead with the person you're helping, not the service.
- Always make the first check easy to say yes to (free, or "just send me who to verify").
- Every happy client = ask for a review **and** a referral. That's the whole growth engine.
