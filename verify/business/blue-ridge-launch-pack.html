Blue Ridge Press LLC — Background-Check Launch Checklist

Working checklist to take the consent-based screening business from "live site" to "official, client-ready." Not legal advice — the FCRA item below needs a real attorney.

⚠️ FIRST — the item that gates everything

  • [ ] Consult a background-screening / FCRA attorney about CRA status. Even consent-based reference / employment / education checks, when a third party uses them for a hiring decision, can legally be "consumer reports" — which can make you a Consumer Reporting Agency under the FCRA, even with no criminal or credit data. Get a clear answer: either "your model stays outside CRA territory, here's how to keep it there," or "you're a CRA, here's how to comply." Do not run a paid check for a real employer until you have this answer.

Phase 1 — Legal foundation

  • [ ] Resolve the entity: confirm with NC Secretary of State which LLC is registered and in good standing (QuickBooks says Blue Ridge Financial Coaching LLC; the business is Blue Ridge Press LLC), and which one the checks run under.
  • [ ] Obtain / confirm EIN for that entity (IRS, free).
  • [ ] File DBA / assumed name if operating under a name ≠ the registered LLC (Wake County Register of Deeds).
  • [ ] Check City of Raleigh / Wake County local business-license requirements.
  • [ ] Attorney-reviewed Privacy Policy + Terms (drafts already live on the site).
  • [ ] FCRA-compliant disclosure + authorization form finalized by counsel (starting draft: 02-consent-disclosure-form.md).
  • [ ] E&O / professional liability insurance (screening-specific) — get quotes.

Phase 2 — Money & operations

  • [x] Billable service items in QuickBooks — $39 check, $149 five-pack.
  • [x] Pay-now link ($39, card/bank).
  • [ ] Dedicated business bank account in the entity name (separate from personal).
  • [ ] Confirm sales-tax treatment (NC generally exempts professional services — verify).
  • [ ] Stand up a repeatable delivery process — deploy the Verify console (secure storage + audit trail) or run the manual Jotform workflow.
  • [ ] Adopt the data-retention & security policy (03-data-retention-security-policy.md).

Phase 3 — Brand & launch

  • [x] Website: Home, Pricing, About, Privacy, Terms, Sample Report, SEO, tagline.
  • [ ] Activate favicon + social image (two uploads).
  • [ ] Confirm all pages render in your browser.
  • [ ] Set up business email (rob@blueridgepressllc.com).
  • [ ] Create Google Business Profile (copy: 04-google-business-profile.md).
  • [ ] Work the first-clients plan (05-first-clients-plan.md).

Suggested order

  1. Attorney call (FCRA/CRA) — books the risk.
  2. In parallel: entity cleanup, EIN, bank account, insurance quotes.
  3. Finalize consent form + policies with counsel.
  4. Turn on delivery (console or manual) + business email.
  5. Google profile live → start the first-clients outreach.

Background Check — Disclosure & Authorization

Blue Ridge Press LLC

INTERNAL NOTE (delete before sending to candidates): This is a plain-language starting draft, not legal advice. Have a background-screening / FCRA attorney review and finalize it before use. If your attorney determines you are a Consumer Reporting Agency, FCRA requires the disclosure to be a standalone document (its own page, no other content, no liability waivers mixed in) and the authorization to be clear and conspicuous. Keep the signed copy with a timestamp.

Part 1 — Disclosure

Blue Ridge Press LLC has been asked by [Requesting Party / Employer] to confirm information you have provided, so they can make an informed decision about [the position / role / engagement].

With your permission, we will contact the sources you provide and confirm:

  • Professional references — your relationship, dates, and whether they recommend you
  • Past employment — role, dates, and (where the employer will say) rehire eligibility
  • Education / credentials — attendance and the degree or credential earned

What we do not do: We do not obtain criminal history, credit reports, or driving records; we are not a consumer-reporting agency for those. We do not search social media or purchase third-party background files. We only confirm what you and your own listed sources attest to.

Nothing is contacted or checked until you sign the authorization below.

Part 2 — Your information

  • Full legal name: __________
  • Preferred email: __________
  • Phone (optional): _________

References / past employers / schools to contact (add as many as apply):

Type (reference / employer / school) Name of person or organization Their email or phone

Part 3 — Authorization

I have read the Disclosure above. I authorize Blue Ridge Press LLC to contact the sources I have listed and to confirm the reference, employment, and education information described. I understand:

  • No source is contacted until I sign this authorization.
  • I may decline, and any source may decline to respond.
  • The completed report is provided only to the requesting party named above.
  • I may ask what information is held about me and request its deletion.

Signature: _____ Date: ______

(Electronic signature and timestamp accepted.)

Data Retention & Security Policy

Blue Ridge Press LLC — internal operating policy

Starting template, not legal advice. Align with your attorney's guidance and any FCRA / state-law requirements that apply to your model.

1. What we collect

  • Client (requesting party): name, business, contact details, and the details of the person to be verified.
  • Candidate: identifying details needed to run the check, the references / employers / schools they provide, and their signed authorization (with timestamp).
  • Sources: the confirmations a reference, employer, or registrar chooses to give.

We do not collect criminal, credit, or driving records, SSNs, or purchased background files.

2. Purpose limitation

Information is used only to perform the requested verification and deliver the report to the requesting party. It is never sold, used for advertising, or used to build profiles.

3. Storage & access

  • Stored in the Verify system (access-controlled) and, where used, our e-signature and email tools.
  • Access is limited to the owner/operator. Anyone with access is bound by this policy.
  • Devices used for the business are password/passphrase protected and, where possible, disk-encrypted.
  • No candidate PII is kept in personal email, texts, spreadsheets, or chat tools.

4. Retention schedule

Record Kept for Then
Signed consent / authorization Duration of engagement + [X] years per counsel Deleted
Completed report Delivery + [X] months for client re-download Deleted
Working notes / draft data Until report delivered Deleted

(Fill in the retention periods your attorney recommends; screening records are often kept a defined number of years for dispute/audit reasons.)

5. Deletion & candidate rights

  • Candidates and clients may ask what is held about them, request corrections, or request deletion.
  • On a valid deletion request (subject to any legal retention obligation), records are removed from the Verify system and connected tools.

6. Breach response

If PII is exposed or lost: (1) contain it, (2) assess what was affected, (3) notify affected people and any parties required by law promptly, (4) fix the cause and record what happened.

7. Vendors

Service providers (e-signature, email, hosting) are used only as needed to run checks and are expected to maintain reasonable security. Review their terms before adding new ones.

Owner: Rob Brizzi. Review this policy at least annually and after any process change.

First-Clients Plan — First 30 Days

Blue Ridge Press LLC · goal: land your first 3–5 paying checks and one referral partner.

You don't need marketing spend. You need 20 real conversations. This is a plan for that.

Who to target first (tightest fit → widest)

  1. Families hiring in-home help for an aging parent — your sharpest wedge and the reason the book exists. They're handing a stranger the keys to Mom's house.
  2. Small business owners making an early hire — restaurants, salons, trades, clinics.
  3. Independent landlords vetting a tenant or a handyman.
  4. Nonprofits / churches / youth & senior programs vetting volunteers.

The one-sentence pitch

"I run honest, consent-based reference checks — I actually call a person's former managers and references and confirm they're who they say — for a fraction of what the big screening companies charge, starting at $39."

Referral partners (the real leverage)

These people meet your buyers every day. One good partner > 100 cold posts. - Home-care & senior-care agencies (they place caregivers; families still want independent vetting) - Elder-law attorneys & geriatric care managers - Property-management companies & realtors - Small-business bookkeepers / accountants (they know every new employer) - Staffing / recruiting micro-agencies without their own screening

The ask: "When you meet someone hiring a caregiver / a first employee / a tenant and they're nervous about who they're trusting, send them my way. Their first check is on me."

30-day plan

Week 1 — set the table - Finish site (favicon, social image, confirm pages render), business email, Google profile. - Write your list: 20 people you already know in the 4 buyer groups + 10 potential partners.

Week 2 — warm outreach - Message all 20 personally (not a blast): "I started an honest, affordable background-check service — mind if I tell you what it does in case you or someone you know is hiring?" - Offer the first check free to 3 people whose feedback + testimonial you want.

Week 3 — partners - Coffee / call with 5 potential referral partners. Bring the one-pager and the free-check offer for their referrals. - Post the caregiver angle where your buyers already are (local parenting/caregiver groups, church bulletins, neighborhood boards) — helpful, not salesy.

Week 4 — convert + prove - Run the free/first checks, deliver clean reports, ask each happy client for: (1) a Google review, (2) a one-line testimonial for the site, (3) one referral. - Send your first real invoice / pay-link. Bank a paying client.

Track it simply

Contact Group Status Next step

The mindset

Your competitor isn't Checkr — it's the busy person who means to call references and never does. Sell "someone actually did the diligence, with consent, for my one hire." Do 5 of those flawlessly and the referrals start doing the selling for you.
