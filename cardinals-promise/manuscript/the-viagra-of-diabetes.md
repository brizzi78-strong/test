Early on — before the shortage, before the hashtags, before a tuxedo said the word to a theater full of famous people — somebody looked at the bag I was carrying and told me what was in it.

"You know what you've got there," he said. "That's going to be the Viagra of diabetes."

I took it as a compliment. I heard it the way a young rep hears everything: as a forecast of numbers. Viagra meant blockbuster. Viagra meant a drug so big that patients walk in asking for it by name, and the selling does itself, and the guy holding the bag in the right territory at the right time gets his name on a list.

I drove to my next call doing that math. I did it happily. I did it all the way to the parking lot of the next office.
