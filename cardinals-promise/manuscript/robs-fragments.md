> scripts, and the staff's getting skinnier. We can't sample here anymore.

**What this anchors (all now TRUE, not stand-in):**

3. "We always stayed on label" — Wegovy was the on-label weight-loss
   version and it was on shortage; Ozempic's weight-loss fame was, from the
   rep's seat, a side effect that swallowed the brand.
4. The book's market thesis in his voice: Novo went from mid-level insulin

## 2026-07-30 — the first line, the exam, and why they hired him

> type one and type two diabetes, and I was about to start training. The
> training process was over the course of three months. I had to take a

> chances — I would lose my job. So talk about going from naive about

> time when pharmaceutical and medical device companies would hire
> pharmacists only, clinical people, nurses — and what they realized was
> they weren't selling, even though these people were super knowledgeable.

> science. And, wow, it worked.

**What this anchors (all TRUE):**

3. The syllabus of the transformation: GLP-1 mechanism of action,

4. The industry's hiring history: clinical hires (pharmacists, nurses)
