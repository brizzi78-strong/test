# Platform Playbooks

Same mission everywhere. Different room, different way of sitting in it.
Reddit has its own file — `reddit-playbook.md` — because Reddit punishes
everything the other platforms reward.

## Facebook — the living room

**Who's there:** the actual audience. Adult daughters and sons, 45–70,
caring for a parent right now. Caregiver groups with tens of thousands of
members.

**How to show up:**
- Personal page/author page: the full weekly rhythm (Map / Unspoken Words / Story).
- Caregiver and hospice groups: join as a helper, not a promoter. Answer
  questions. Most groups ban links — respect it. Your name and profile do
  the work; the profile links to thecardinalspromise.com.
- Longer posts work here. 150–300 words. One photo beats a graphic; a real
  photo (the folder, the trifold, a cardinal, Lou) beats stock every time.

**Cadence:** 3 posts/week + daily group participation when a question fits.

## Instagram — the window

**Who's there:** younger caregivers, hospice nurses, social workers, the
sandwich generation.

**How to show up:**
- Carousel posts for the Unspoken Words (one phrase per slide, plain type,
  lots of white space — the words are the design).
- Single-image quote cards for Map tips using the book's color system
  (RED / PURPLE / BLUE).
- Reels: 30–60 seconds, Rob talking to camera, one truth per video. No
  music over bedside content. Captions always on.
- Stories: repost the day's content, answer DMs.

**Cadence:** 3 feed posts/week, Reels when there's something true to say.
Link in bio → thecardinalspromise.com.

## LinkedIn — the referral floor

**Who's there:** the people who make the call. Discharge planners, social
workers, DONs, senior living operators, home health and hospice colleagues.

**How to show up:**
- Wednesday post aimed at providers: the weight of every referral, language
  for the hospice conversation, what families wish the care team had said.
- Behind the Promise pillar lands hardest here — the work, witnessed.
- Plain text posts outperform graphics. First two lines carry everything.
- This is also where speaking engagements come from. Every post is a
  quiet audition for a keynote.

**Cadence:** 2 posts/week. Comment genuinely on hospice/senior-care posts daily.

## X / Threads — the single sentence

**Who's there:** fast scrollers. Some healthcare community (#hpm — hospice
and palliative medicine — lives on X).

**How to show up:**
- One line at a time. The pillars compress well: a single Unspoken Words
  phrase is a complete post here.
- Thread format for the Map content: "7 things to put in one folder before
  the crisis. 🧵"
- Repost the same content to both platforms; effort stays low.

**Cadence:** 3–5/week, mostly recycled from the day's main post.

## TikTok / YouTube Shorts — the kitchen table

**Who's there:** an enormous, underserved caregiver audience. "Nobody told
me this about hospice" content regularly reaches millions because nobody
with real experience is making it.

**How to show up:**
- Face to camera. Kitchen table or car. No production.
- Formats that work:
  - "Words for the moment nobody prepares you for" (Unspoken Words, said
    slowly, once)
  - "What a hospice guy wants every family to know" (Map + reframe)
  - "The question every discharge planner hopes you'll ask" (Map)
- One video = one truth. 45 seconds. End with silence, not a CTA.
- The book comes up when commenters ask. They will ask.

**Cadence:** 1–2/week to start. Same video posted to TikTok, Reels, Shorts.

## YouTube (long form) — the archive

**Who's there:** people searching at 2am: "how do I know when it's time
for hospice," "what to say to someone who is dying."

**How to show up:**
- 5–10 minute talks, one per Toolkit chapter theme. Searchable titles that
  match the 2am question word for word.
- Description links to the site and the book. This is the one platform
  where linking is expected and welcome.

**Cadence:** 1–2/month. These compound for years.

## Everywhere: the reply rule

The post is the door. The reply is the room. Someone who comments "my mom
was just referred to hospice and I'm scared" gets a real answer within the
day — every platform, every time. That reply is the mission. The post was
just how they found the door.
