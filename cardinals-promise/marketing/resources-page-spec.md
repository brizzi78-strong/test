# Spec — TheCardinalsPromise.com/resources
*The page every KEEP GOING QR code in the printed book points to. The book is
the front door; this page is the hallway to everything else.*

## The one rule
**This URL must never break.** It is printed in ink in a book with a long
shelf life. The path `/resources` can redirect anywhere later, but it must
always resolve. Set the redirect up before the book ships, even if it points
to the homepage at first.

## What the reader was promised
Every QR block in the book says exactly:
> Free tools, current links & printable resources

So the page must deliver those three things above the fold, in that order,
before it sells anything.

## Page structure (top to bottom)

1. **Free printable worksheets** — `free-worksheets.pdf` (built: medication
   & doctor list, emergency contacts, documents folder checklist, assisted
   living tour sheet). One click, no email wall. The email ask sits *beside*
   the download, never in front of it.
2. **The complete companion workbook** — all 25 sheets
   (`its-not-your-fault-workbook.pdf`, built). Free or book-bonus — Rob's
   pricing call, pending.
3. **Current links & numbers** — the maintained list the book deliberately
   doesn't freeze in print: Medicare/SHIP, Medicaid/DSS, VA/VSO + Aid &
   Attendance, Eldercare Locator (800-677-1116), Alzheimer's Helpline
   (800-272-3900), 988. Each with a "last reviewed" date so readers can
   trust it's alive.
4. **Cardinal Care Bridge** — placement & care navigation, no cost to
   families. Short intro + contact/intake. This is the page's primary
   service conversion.
5. **Email signup** — "one useful thing a week for caregivers." Kit form
   (account already connected).
6. **The books** — *It's Not Your Fault* (buy links) and *The Cardinal's
   Promise* (the memoir — the heart, for when they're ready).
7. **Coming** — the app ($4.99/mo, expanded depth; not promised in print
   until live), speaking, the mission.

## The funnel this page serves
Book → /resources → free tools → email relationship → Care Bridge /
workbook / app → the Cardinal's Promise mission.

## Maintenance contract
- Review links quarterly; update the "last reviewed" stamps.
- Never remove `/resources`; only redirect it.
- When benefit numbers change (look-back, Part D, etc.), this page updates
  the same week — that currency is the book's competitive moat over
  out-of-date rivals.
