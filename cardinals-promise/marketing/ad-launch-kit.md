# Ad Launch Kit — It's Not Your Fault

> Built for a book that is not yet live. **Ads amplify a listing; they don't fix one.**
> Do Phase 0 first — get the book live and land 5–10 honest reviews — or paid clicks
> hit a page that can't convert and the money is wasted.

**Audience truth:** the buyer is usually an **adult daughter, 45–65**, caring for a
parent, searching at night, scared and overwhelmed. Every ad speaks to *her*, in the
book's voice: *you're not failing, you just haven't been shown how.*

**The tested hook (leads everything):** **"What do I do with my aging parent?"** —
answered by the title, *It's Not Your Fault.*

---

## Phase 0 — Before a dollar is spent
- [ ] Book live on KDP, description + categories set
- [ ] **5–10 honest reviews** (beta readers, attorney-program recipients, family network) — the single biggest lever on ad ROI
- [ ] A+ Content / brand story added in KDP (free, lifts conversion)
- [ ] Author Central profile claimed

---

## Channel 1 — Amazon Ads (start here: highest intent, cheapest)

Buyers are already shopping. This is where book ad dollars work hardest. Run inside
KDP → Marketing → Advertising, or advertising.amazon.com.

### Campaign A — Auto (discovery) · $10/day
- Type: Sponsored Products, **Automatic targeting**
- Bidding: **Dynamic bids — down only**
- Default bid: **$0.35**, adjust after data
- Purpose: let Amazon find converting search terms; harvest the winners into Campaign B after 2 weeks.

### Campaign B — Manual keywords · $10–15/day
- Type: Sponsored Products, **Manual → Keyword targeting**
- Match: run **phrase** and **exact** (skip broad at first to control spend)
- Starting bid: **$0.40–0.60** (books are cheap clicks)
- Seed keywords:
  - caring for elderly parents
  - aging parents help
  - caregiver book
  - dementia caregiver guide
  - elder care planning
  - how to care for aging parents
  - sandwich generation
  - hospice family guide
  - power of attorney elderly parent
  - medicaid long term care
  - caregiver burnout
  - assisted living decision

### Campaign C — Product/ASIN targeting (add week 3) · $10/day
- Type: Sponsored Products, **Manual → Product targeting**
- Target the detail pages of comparable titles (your ad shows *on their listing*):
  - *The 36-Hour Day*
  - *How to Care for Aging Parents*
  - *Being Mortal* (Atul Gawande)
  - *Can't We Talk About Something More Pleasant?*
  - *A Bittersweet Season*
  - *The Caregiver's Encyclopedia*

### Negative keywords (add to A & B)
free, pdf, kindle unlimited, jobs, salary, certification, course, resume

### How to run it
- Give it **2 weeks / ~$300** before judging — Amazon needs data.
- Watch **ACOS** (ad cost ÷ sales). For a first book, **breaking even on ACOS is fine** — you're buying rank and reviews, not just today's royalty.
- Every 3–4 days: pause keywords with lots of clicks and zero sales; raise bids on keywords that convert; move winning auto search-terms into Campaign B as exact.

## Channel 2 — Meta Ads (Facebook / Instagram: emotional reach)

Your audience lives on Facebook. Objective: **Traffic** to the Amazon listing (or a
cardinalspromise.com landing page — higher converting, see Channel 4). Start **$15–20/day**.

### Audience
- **Location:** United States
- **Age:** 45–65 · **Gender:** All (skew female in results, don't pre-exclude men)
- **Detailed targeting (interests):** Caregiver · AARP · Alzheimer's Association · Aging in place · Assisted living · Hospice · Dementia · Family caregivers · Elder law
- **Later:** build a **Lookalike** from the pixel/page engagers once traffic flows
- **Placements:** Advantage+ (let Meta optimize), or manual: FB Feed, IG Feed, Stories

### Ad copy variants (test 3–4 at once, $5/day each, kill losers)

**1 — The midnight question**
> It's 2 a.m. and you're Googling "what do I do with my aging parent?"
> You're not failing. Nobody handed you a map. This book is the map.
> *It's Not Your Fault: Helping Caregivers with Aging Loved Ones.*

**2 — Permission**
> You didn't sign up for this. A fall, a diagnosis, a discharge planner asking where
> Mom goes next — and somehow you're supposed to know.
> A Certified Dementia Practitioner wrote the guide he wished every family had.

**3 — The color-code**
> 29 chapters, color-coded by urgency — RED: act today · PURPLE: watch closely · BLUE: plan ahead.
> Find the right page in under a minute, on your worst day.

**4 — The burnout angle**
> A plan that protects your parent but breaks you is not a real plan.
> This book helps you care for them *and* survive it.

**5 — The scam/finances angle (Clark Howard tie-in)**
> A missed bill. A withdrawal she can't explain. A scammer who finally got through.
> Sometimes the checkbook shows what a face still hides. Learn what to watch for.

### Creative direction (no fabricated faces/testimonials)
- **Hero image:** the book cover on a warm, softly-lit table (real object shot) — converts well and stays honest.
- **Text-card ad:** cream background, navy type, the midnight-question hook — cheap to make, matches the brand.
- **Short video (15s):** slow pan over the open workbook pages + the hook as captions; the color-coding is visually distinctive.
- Avoid stock "sad old person" clichés — they read as insurance ads and underperform.

## Channel 3 — The channel you already own (don't sleep on it)

The **elder-law attorney / financial-advisor bulk + bookplate program** (already built:
sell sheet, outreach emails, Triangle prospect list) is higher-value than paid clicks.
One firm buying 50 copies at a bulk rate beats hundreds of $0.50 clicks — and it's a
warm, repeatable channel. Work this in parallel with the ads.

---

## Channel 4 — Landing page (lifts every paid click)

Cold traffic converts better on a page you control than on a bare Amazon listing.
Simple cardinalspromise.com page: the hook → what the book is → one **Buy on Amazon**
button → optional free-worksheet email capture (feeds a Kit welcome sequence). Send
Meta traffic here; send Amazon Ads straight to the listing.

## 90-Day rollout & budget (adjust to comfort)

| Weeks | Focus | Daily spend | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Launch + first reviews | $0 | 5–10 reviews, listing dialed in |
| 1–2 | Amazon Auto + Manual | ~$20 | find converting keywords |
| 3–4 | Add Amazon Product-targeting; launch Meta test | ~$40 | first repeatable ROAS signal |
| 5–8 | Scale winners, cut losers; build Lookalike | ~$50–75 | profitable/break-even ACOS |
| 9–12 | Double winning ad sets; push attorney channel | scale to comfort | steady rank + B2B orders |

**Honest guardrail on "massive":** don't scale a channel until it shows a repeatable
return at small spend. $50/day that converts beats $500/day that doesn't. Scale the
winners; don't front-load the budget.
