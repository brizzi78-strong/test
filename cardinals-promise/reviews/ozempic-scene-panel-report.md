# Panel Report — "The Question in the Hallway" (Ozempic scene)

Scene: `manuscript/one-reps-story-ozempic.md` · Draft v1 reviewed 2026-07-29 by a
three-reviewer panel. Fixes marked **applied** are in draft v2 (same file);
items marked **for Rob** need his memory, not an editor.

## The panel

| Lens | Reviewer charge |
|---|---|
| Craft / voice | Line-level check against the book's voice rules (show don't tell, no teaching tone, silence does work) |
| Pharma authenticity | Would a real Novo diabetes-care rep and a real PCP office recognize this? Compliance, timeline, clinical plausibility |
| Mission alignment | Does the patient stay the center? Is the hospice bridge earned? Would a grieving or chronically ill reader trust it? |

## Major findings → what was done

| # | Finding | Reviewer(s) | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Title "How I Sold Ozempic" is a sales marquee** — frames every line as technique; skimmers and shares receive exactly what the mission forbids | Mission | **Applied** — retitled "The Question in the Hallway"; the original phrase demoted to an italic deck |
| 2 | **"One click" is the competitor's pitch** — that was Trulicity's autoinjector; the Ozempic pen is dial-a-dose with a visible (tiny) needle. Any former diabetes rep catches it instantly | Pharma | **Applied** — replaced with the real Novo talk track (needle thinner than an eyelash) |
| 3 | **"Send me the prior auth" is a HIPAA/compliance violation** — a rep can educate staff on the PA process but never receive or file it; same for writing "the appeal letter" | Pharma | **Applied** — rep now walks staff through the PA and brings a *template* appeal letter; "That's my job" → "That part's on us, not her" |
| 4 | **Six-week A1C recheck is clinically impossible** — A1C reflects ~3 months; Ozempic titration means only two therapeutic doses by week 6 | Pharma | **Applied** — "Three months later" (also matches a rep's real call cycle) |
| 5 | **Timeline collides with COVID** — "two years" after the Feb 2018 launch puts the hallway scene in spring 2020, when NJ offices had locked reps out | Pharma | **Applied** — "a year and a half," scene set fall 2019; flagged for Rob to confirm the real year |
| 6 | **The shame resolution credited the drug** — good lab number → smile is the Ozempic-ad arc; nine years of apologizing doesn't end because a value starts with a seven | Mission | **Applied** — the doctor now says the unspoken words ("you have never once let me down; you have a disease"), and she *catches herself* mid-apology instead of beaming. The number stays; the healing is human |
| 7 | **Frictionless miracle arc** — no nausea, no cost fight, no ambivalence reads as a testimonial | Pharma + Mission | **Applied** — one line of real friction (rough first month, two nausea calls) and a confessional beat (the question worked, and it paid the mortgage — caring or closing?) |
| 8 | **The ending preached and contradicted the scene** — "Show up. Ask… Then stay" is a keynote tricolon, and "stay" is exactly what the rep didn't do (he drove to the next office) | Craft + Mission | **Applied** — bridge rebuilt as a correction, not a continuation: "That was the job then: ask, and leave… Hospice taught me what comes after. I still ask the question. Now I stay for the answer." |
| 9 | **The "How to…" paragraph was curriculum, not memoir** — three anaphoric clauses, stated morals ("it's never really about the needle" — glib to actual needle-phobic readers) | Craft + Mission | **Applied** — paragraph cut entirely |
| 10 | **The patient's story arrived as narrator summary** — the emotional core (the mother who went blind) was compressed gloss instead of the doctor's own speech | Craft + Mission | **Applied** — moved into the doctor's mouth with the inheritance beat ("she watched her mother take the shots the whole way down") |
| 11 | **Self-congratulation drift** — "the part that mattered more," the requoted mic-drop close (which also misquoted the original question), "No visual aid. No close." | Craft + Mission | **Applied** — flags and restatement cut; sample-signature beat added instead (the doctor signs unprompted — the reversal shown, not told) |
| 12 | **Consistency** — 26 vs. 30 patients; question wording drift between ask and requote | Craft + Pharma + Mission | **Applied** — 26 throughout; requote removed |
