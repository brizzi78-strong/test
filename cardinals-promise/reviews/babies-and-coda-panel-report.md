## Ozempic Babies — findings → what was done

| # | Finding | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **MEDICAL, highest-stakes in either piece:** the draft implied Ozempic weakens oral contraceptives — that warning is **tirzepatide's** (Mounjaro/Zepbound label: backup contraception at initiation and dose increases); semaglutide's label carries no OC warning and its interaction studies were clean | **Applied** — "one of them, Ozempic's younger cousin, carries a warning right on its label"; the distinction locked into the confirm list |
| 2 | "I want to be honest about the other side… because the book has been honest about every other one" — the performing-disclaimer tic PLUS the book grading itself | **Applied** — cut; the pivot opens on "A surprise pregnancy is only good news when it's good news." |
| 3 | "The wave had undertow. Waves do." — aphorism in the struck two-word cadence; undertow got checkbox treatment | **Applied** — cut; replaced with one concrete image (counting backward on a calendar to the doses taken before the test) |
| 4 | "I know exactly what a life that almost didn't happen is worth" — "exactly" overclaims and captions sacred material | **Applied** — cut; the adoption facts stand bare and the reader connects them |
| 5 | The door-hinges conceit (second door metaphor, weight-loss pun, backwards physics) + "Two doors, quietly unlocked" | **Applied** — both cut; "Her body did the rest." full stop |
| 6 | Caption ending ("It felt like the truest thing I'd said about that drug in years") | **Applied** — ends on "Congratulations," I said. |
| 7 | "including the company" (unverifiable corporate interiority); résumé inventory before the sonogram; duplicate epigraph claim | **Applied** — all trimmed |

Adjudicated keeps: "She showed me a sonogram." as its own paragraph; the
eleven-years speech and the laugh "when the thing is still too big to say
plainly"; the husband "who had stopped trying to arrange his face weeks
ago" (good, not showing off); "more people in the room instead of fewer"
(the book's room motif doing real work); the adoption paragraph's
*placement* (it answers why the wave lands on Rob, and she gets the last
exchange). "Stop about two months before trying" verified accurate for
semaglutide; "hundreds of women" conservative vs. the reported ~500+ group.
