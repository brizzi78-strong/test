# Panel Report — Book Proposal, "The Other Ledger"

Document: `publishing/book-proposal-other-ledger.md` · Draft v1 reviewed
2026-07-30 through three lenses (skeptical literary agent, fact-checker,
mission ethics). Fixes marked **applied** are in draft v2.

## Bottom-line agent verdict (v1)

**"Would request the full — would not yet offer."** What holds it at
"request": a platform section that was all brackets, sample chapters that
quietly contained invented scenes, and arithmetic that didn't survive a
second read. What converts it to "offer," in order: certified-lived
samples; one real platform number; the #1-ranking claim filled and exact;
The In-Between on the comps list; a query timed to a 2026–27 news peg with
an op-ed already placed.

## Verified by the fact-checker (kept)

*Hard Sell* → *Love & Other Drugs* (2010); *Magic Pill* (Hari, 2024);
*Dopesick* (Macy, 2018); *Being Mortal* (Gawande, 2014); IRA-negotiated
semaglutide prices effective Jan 2027. Post-2025 items (orforglipron 2026,
Medicare cardiovascular expansion July 2026) rest on trade-press sources in
`chapter-gaps.md` — independently verify before submission.

## Kept on merit

The opening sentence (the pen in the bag in the trunk); the insider
triangle; the legal-posture paragraph ("worth real money and time at
vetting — a genuine differentiator"); the audience section's "help desk
readers"; the butter-pecan sentence ("this is the book").
