# De-AI Change Log — Chapter Openers

> Audit finding: 85 em-dashes in 5,381 words (15.8 per 1,000; human norm
> is under 4, and 8+ is the strongest single machine-writing signal).
> Vocabulary, phrases, and parallelism came back essentially clean. This
> pass is therefore punctuation surgery: 62 edits, almost all em-dash
> replacements (periods, commas, colons, and a few parentheses — which
> humans use and models rarely do). New density: 1.1 per 1,000, with
> five dashes deliberately kept where they earn their place.

## Deliberately kept (real voice, do not fix)

- Ch. 3: "Do you mean they're working with you — because of the therapy?" (spoken pause in dialogue)
- Ch. 27: "she was held — the family was in town" (spoken beat)
- Ch. 27: "still learning to believe about myself — and I put it on the cover..." (the closer)
- Ch. 29: "his wishes — he wanted to be buried" (spoken beat)
- Ch. 29: "January 9th — seventeen years, to the day, since I got sober — a cardinal landed" (the reveal)
- Protected "not X / it is Y" lines kept intact: "not a longer life. It is a harder death," the hospice creed, "It is *him*," "not a recovery. It is a gift," "never win an argument with dementia."

## Non-punctuation edits (3)

- Ch. 10: "A significant number of people" → "A lot of people" (last AI-vocab hit; plainer, same claim)
- Ch. 10: "That is not pessimism. That is showing up prepared." → "That isn't pessimism. That's being prepared." (spoken register)
- Chs. 5, 24: the two semicolons became periods (nobody speaks a semicolon)
