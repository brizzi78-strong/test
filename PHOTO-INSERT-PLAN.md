# The Cardinal's Promise — Photo Insert Plan

## Concept

An 8-page black-and-white photo insert. Every image chosen is already
*described in the manuscript text* — the book has effectively written its own
captions. That's the test for inclusion: if the text doesn't reference it, it
doesn't go in.

## Placement

Between Part Four (Getting Square) and Part Five (Hope) — the traditional
mid-book position, and thematically the hinge: the reader has just watched the
rebuild and is about to meet Hope. Alternative: after the Epilogue, before
"For Those Walking This Road" (keeps the narrative unbroken; photos land as a
coda). Default recommendation: mid-book.

## A note on recreated images

Two images in hand are illustrations/AI-style recreations rather than
original photographs (the Lou picnic-table illustration; possibly the
mother-and-child Mexico street image). In a memoir, recreations must not be
presented as photographs — if used, label plainly (e.g., "illustration from
a family photograph"). Default: use the real photo where one exists; keep
recreations for marketing/web, not the printed insert.

## Technical (KDP)

- Interior is B&W: all photos convert to grayscale. Check each for contrast
  after conversion — the older snapshots may need levels adjustment.
- 300 DPI at print size; no bleed needed if images sit inside the text-safe
  margin block.
- **Page-count impact**: locked interior is 226 pp; +8 pp insert = 234 pp.
  Spine width changes from 0.509" — the cover wrap in KDP-COVER-SPECS.md must
  be recalculated before the cover is finalized. Do NOT add the insert after
  the cover is locked.
- EPUB: images can be included in color at no printing cost — recommended.
- Speechify/TTS build: exclude the insert (image-only pages, no text impact).
