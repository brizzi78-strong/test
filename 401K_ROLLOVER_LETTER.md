# 401(k) Rollover Letters — Robinhood IRA → Amedisys 401(k) at Merrill

Two short letters for the incoming rollover. Fill in the bracketed blanks, print, and sign.

**Before mailing anything, confirm by phone with Merrill (1-866-820-1492) that:**

1. The plan accepts an incoming direct rollover from a rollover Traditional IRA.
2. The exact check payee line is still current.
3. The mailing address (Pennington, NJ) is still current — and ask for the
   overnight/street address so the check can be sent by tracked overnight delivery.
4. What proof they require that the IRA originated from a former employer 401(k).
