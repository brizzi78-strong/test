# The Cardinal — First 100 Launch Playbook

**The one principle:** density beats reach. A social network is only alive if, when
someone shows up, their people are already there. So don't launch to 100 strangers —
launch to **one tight community that already knows each other**, all in the same week.

---

## Step 1 — Make a list of ~150 real people you actually know
(Aim high; expect ~100 to actually stick.) Pull from the circles where people already
have real relationships:

- **Family & close friends** — the ones who'll join just because *you* asked
- **Your faith / church community**
- **Neighbors & your town** (Raleigh / Blue Ridge area)
- **A club, team, board, or group** you're part of
- **Former colleagues** who'd champion it

Then mark your **inner ring**: the 10–15 who will *definitely* join and actually post.
They go first. Everything depends on them.

## Step 2 — Seed it so it's never empty
Before anyone else arrives, you + your inner ring put up a few real things — a hello, a
couple of photos, one genuine "Reach out." An empty feed feels dead; a warm one invites
people in. (The app already has a demo-seed that shows exactly what "alive" looks like.)

## Step 3 — Invite in waves, not all at once
Each wave should walk into an **active** feed.

- **Wave 1 — Day 1:** your inner ring (10–15). Personal text or call, never a mass blast.
- **Wave 2 — Week 1:** the next 30–40, once Wave 1 is posting.
- **Wave 3 — Weeks 2–3:** the rest of the 100.

## Step 4 — Make the first week about *showing up*, not signing up
Your hook isn't the feed — it's **"Reach out."** Encourage one real moment where someone
raises a hand and their circle shows up. A single genuine act of support will do more for
retention than a hundred posts.

## Step 5 — One invite each
Ask every member to invite **1–2 people they'd genuinely want there.** Controlled,
word-of-mouth growth keeps it real and verified — the opposite of Facebook's free-for-all.

---

## The invite script (copy/paste)
> Hey [name] — I built a small social network called **The Cardinal**. No ads, no
> algorithm, just real people and your actual circle — a calmer place to stay close.
> I'm inviting a handful of people I actually care about first. Want in? [link]

## What to measure
**Not** signups. Count **posts and "reach-outs" in week one.** If people post and show up
for each other, it's working. If they sign up and vanish, tighten the circle — go smaller,
more personal, before you go wider.

---

## One dependency to plan around
Right now only the landing page ("Launching soon") is live. To invite people to *use* it,
the app itself needs to be online. Two ways to sequence it:

1. **Waitlist first** — collect interested emails now while it says "Launching soon,"
   then invite them in waves once the app is deployed. (Lowest effort to start today.)
2. **Deploy first** — stand the app up on a small server (the one-command deploy), then
   run Steps 1–5 for real.

Either works. The list-building in Steps 1–2 you can start **today**, regardless.
