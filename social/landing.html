Why it exists

Every other network was built to keep you scrolling. This one was built to keep you close.

i.

Everyone here is real.

Every member verifies their real identity before they arrive — a real name, a real face, a person who can actually be reached. No bots, no burner accounts, no anonymous strangers.

ii.

Your feed is your circle.

Chronological, finite, and yours. When you've seen your people, you're caught up — no bottomless scroll, no algorithm deciding who to make you mad at today.

iii.

You show up for each other.

Raise a quiet signal when you're having a hard time, and the people who love you are the first to know. Presence isn't a feature here. It's the whole point.

iv.

Nothing here is for sale.

No ads. No feed auction. We don't sell your attention or your data — not now, not ever. You are the member, never the product.

The heart of it

When someone needs you, you'll know.

Anyone can quietly raise a hand — “I could use some support.” Only your circle sees it, right at the top of their day, with one gentle way to answer.

The digital version of showing up on someone's porch.
