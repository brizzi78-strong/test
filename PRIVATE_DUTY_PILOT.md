# 90-day referral pilot — Cardinal Care Bridge × [private duty company]

A one-page working agreement to test referral flow between a Triangle private
duty home care company and Cardinal Care Bridge placement, **before** any
equity, joint venture, or money is discussed. Companion to
[`PLACEMENT_GROWTH_PLAN.md`](PLACEMENT_GROWTH_PLAN.md) and
[`PLACEMENT_COMPENSATION.md`](PLACEMENT_COMPENSATION.md).

## Why a pilot first

The partnership thesis: private duty and placement serve the **same families at
different moments**. When an in-home client declines past what aides can
handle, the home care company loses the client and earns nothing on the exit.
When a family calls placement too early ("she just needs help at home"), the
lead dies. Cross-referral means each side monetizes the leads the other can't
serve — and the home care company's caregivers see "it's time" coming weeks
before any hospital does.

That thesis might be right. The pilot measures it instead of assuming it, and
prices any future JV on **90 days of data instead of enthusiasm at a coffee.**

## Terms (fill in and sign — one page, both signatures)

| Term | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Parties | Cardinal Care Bridge LLC / [company name], owned by Dr. [name] |
| Duration | 90 days from [date], then meet within 14 days to review |
| Money | **None, in either direction.** No referral fees, no revenue share, no equity. |
| Referrals to Cardinal | Families whose care needs exceed in-home support; logged before first Cardinal contact |
| Referrals to [company] | Cardinal inquiries not ready for placement; logged before first [company] contact |
| Logging | Shared sheet: date, family initials, source (aide / liaison / office), outcome, days to outcome |
| Family disclosure | Each side tells the family, unprompted, that the companies refer to each other and that the family is free to use anyone |
| Client ownership | Each family belongs to themselves. No exclusivity, no non-compete between the parties |
| Exit | Either side may end the pilot with 7 days' notice, no reason needed |

## What gets measured

- Referrals sent, each direction (raw count is the headline number)
- Conversion: placement referrals → move-ins; home-care referrals → started service
- Lead quality: days from referral to decision, care-level match accuracy
- **Where they came from**: aide-in-home vs. liaison vs. office — this tells you
  which channel the JV would actually be buying

## Decision rule at day 90

| Outcome | Next step |
| --- | --- |
| Real flow both ways | Negotiate the JV — equity in the placement entity, priced on the data |
| Flow one way only | Stay at handshake referrals; no equity for a one-way channel |
| No flow | Shake hands, stay friends, cost: $0 |

## Guardrails (non-negotiable, and they protect him as much as us)

1. **No per-referral payments — especially with a physician owner.** Profit
   from a future JV comes from ownership of the whole entity, never per head.
   Per-referral payments to or from a physician-owned company reopen the
   fee-splitting / kickback questions this structure exists to avoid.
2. **His patients are not a pipeline.** Any medical practice of Dr. [name]
   stays fully separate from the referral flow. Company-to-company only.
3. **The Lou test governs both sides.** If home care is genuinely better for
   the family, Cardinal says so and refers out — and vice versa. A pilot that
   pressures transitions in either direction is worse than no pilot.
4. **Attorney review before the JV, not before the pilot.** A no-money mutual
   referral handshake is the cleanest arrangement there is; the equity step is
   where counsel comes in. It's on the standing attorney agenda.

## Open items to establish at the first conversation

- [ ] Any referral relationship between [company] and Amedisys (home health or
      hospice)? If the companies trade referrals, the conflict-of-interest
      disclosure question changes shape and goes to the attorney first.
- [ ] Who actually works demand day-to-day — the liaison's name, route, and
      whether placement fits their existing SNF/hospital visits
- [ ] His time horizon: advisor who owns a channel, or operator who wants a
      second business?
