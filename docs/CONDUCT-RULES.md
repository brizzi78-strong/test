# Hardline Rules — CARD

Bright-line rules for the conduct of the Cardinals Promise token project.

These are written as absolutes on purpose. A rule that requires judgment in
the moment is a rule that fails in the moment, because the moment you are
tempted to reason around one of these is exactly the moment it is protecting
you. If a situation seems to call for an exception, that is the signal to
call counsel — not to make the call yourself.

Nothing here is legal advice. These rules encode conservative positions
taken deliberately; counsel should review them and may tighten them further.

---

## I. Selling

**1. The issuer never sells CARD. Not any amount, not ever, not to anyone.**

Not on a DEX, not over the counter, not privately, not to cover costs, not
to pay a lawyer, not in an emergency. If the project needs money, it is
funded from outside the token or it is not funded.

**2. No pre-sale, no private round, no allocation in exchange for anything of value.**

No early access, no discounted purchase, no tokens exchanged for services,
promotion, or advice.

**3. Nobody acting on the issuer's behalf sells either.**

This includes family, friends, and anyone given tokens by the issuer.

---

## II. Speech

**4. Never state, imply, predict, or speculate about price or value.**

Not up, not down, not "undervalued," not "still early," not a chart, not an
emoji standing in for any of these. No exceptions for private messages.

**5. Never tell anyone to buy, and never give a condition under which they should.**

"Only buy if…" is a recommendation with extra steps. The answer to "should I
buy this?" is no, permanently, in every channel, to every person, including
after launch when it feels rude.

**6. Never connect buying CARD to the book, the mission, hospice, grief, or family legacy.**

The audience that trusts the author emotionally is the audience that must
never be sold to. This rule protects them first and the issuer second.

**7. Publish only what is independently verifiable.**

Contract address, supply, transaction hashes, lock expiry, donation records,
risk warnings, source code. If a statement cannot be checked by a stranger
against the blockchain or the repository, it does not get published.

**8. Never promise future work, features, listings, partnerships, or plans.**

The token is finished at deployment. Saying otherwise re-creates the
"efforts of others" the renouncement was designed to remove.

---

## III. Treasury

**9. Treasury tokens are donated to nonprofits and are never sold by the issuer.**

**10. Donations follow a schedule published in advance.**

The schedule is fixed before the first transfer. Discretion in timing is
what makes a treasury look like an issuer position; a published schedule
removes it.

**11. Every donation is recorded and published: date, recipient, amount, and fair market value at transfer.**

**12. No side agreements with recipients about what they do with donated tokens.**

Asking a charity to hold is an attempt to retain control. Once donated, it
is theirs without conditions.

---

## IV. Structure and keys

**13. Deployment happens through an entity, not a personal name.**

Formed before anything touches mainnet.

**14. No personal funds of anyone other than the issuer touch the launch.**

Specifically: no spouse's account, no family money, no borrowed funds.
Shared funding creates shared liability regardless of anyone's good faith.

**15. Launch keys live on a hardware wallet. Never in a browser extension, never on a daily-use machine, never photographed, never typed into any website.**

**16. The seed phrase is recorded offline and its location is documented with the issuer's estate paperwork.**

A wallet nobody else can reach is a treasury that dies with its owner and
charities that are never paid.

**17. LP tokens are locked through an established locker before launch is announced, and the lock is published.**

---

## V. Records

**18. Keep everything, dated: communications about the token, every "don't buy" exchange, donation transfers, tax events, and decisions taken.**

Good faith is not assumed by anyone. It is demonstrated from records or it
is not demonstrated.

**19. Report every taxable event honestly and on time, using a CPA experienced with digital assets.**

---

## VI. Changing these rules

**20. These rules are not amended without written advice from counsel, and the amendment is published with its date.**

**21. When a situation is not covered here, the answer is "not yet" until counsel says otherwise.**

Doing nothing is always available and is never the thing that creates
liability.

---

## The one-line version

Never sell it. Never talk about what it is worth. Never invite anyone in.
Publish everything that can be checked. Write down what you did.
