# CARD governance — spoken summary

The token is Cardinals Promise, ticker CARD. There is one token, one contract, and one
official token site: cp17.org.

There are 250 million CARD total. One hundred million goes into the public pool, one hundred
million stays in the founder's disclosed, unlocked wallet, and fifty million remains in the
published treasury Safe.

Ordinary transfers charge two percent and send it to that treasury. Transfers into or out
of the treasury are exempt. The rate, destination, and exemption cannot be changed.

The founder holding is not locked. That is the biggest risk in the design. The current
policy is no founder sales while pool liquidity is below one hundred thousand dollars. If
that threshold is ever reached, any sale follows a public schedule and a small quarterly
cap. That policy is a promise, not a smart-contract lock.

No board, grant, charity percentage, partner network, or legal entity is described as real
until it actually exists and has the required written, legal, and tax work behind it. The
book remains separate: buying the book does not provide CARD, and buying CARD is not a
donation.

The launch order is: counsel and tax review, independent contract review, Sepolia buy and
sell, verified source, exact pool balance, LP lock, ownership renouncement, and only then a
public contract address at cp17.org.
