# CARD governance — spoken summary

The token is Cardinals Promise, ticker CARD. There is one token, one contract, and one
official token site: cp17.org.

There are one billion CARD total, minted once at deployment. Two hundred million goes into
the public pool. The other eight hundred million — eighty percent — is the founder's
allocation, and it does not go to the founder. It goes into a separate vesting contract
that releases nothing for the first ninety days and then releases continuously over three
years.

That contract has no early-withdrawal function, no acceleration function, and no owner
privilege that shortens the term. The founder cannot take those tokens ahead of schedule,
and neither can anyone else. The schedule is fixed when the contract is deployed and anyone
can read it on-chain. This is a lock, not a promise: the earlier design had an unlocked
founder holding governed by a written policy, and that policy has been replaced by code.

There is no treasury. The supply is the pool and the vesting contract, and nothing else.
Any charitable giving the founder does comes from tokens that have actually vested and is
personal, discretionary, and not promised to anyone.

CARD charges no transfer fee. A transfer of any amount moves exactly that amount. There is
no mint function, no blacklist, and no pause switch, and ownership of the token contract is
renounced after deployment, so no one — including the founder — can change any of this
afterwards.

The founder holds eighty percent of supply. That is a large concentration and it is the
most important fact about this token. It is disclosed everywhere the token is described,
together with the vesting terms and the address of the vesting contract, so that anyone can
verify the lock rather than take anyone's word for it.

No board, grant, charity percentage, partner network, or legal entity is described as real
until it actually exists and has the required written, legal, and tax work behind it. The
book remains separate: buying the book does not provide CARD, and buying CARD is not a
donation.

The launch order is: counsel and tax review, independent contract review, Sepolia buy and
sell, verified source for both contracts, exact pool balance, LP lock, ownership
renouncement, and only then a public contract address at cp17.org.
