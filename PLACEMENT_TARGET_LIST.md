# Community target list — Wake, Durham, Orange (first pass)

The supply side of [`PLACEMENT_GROWTH_PLAN.md`](PLACEMENT_GROWTH_PLAN.md): the
communities to sign referral agreements with, grouped so a Saturday drive
covers a cluster. Goal is 25–40 signed agreements in six weeks at ~10 contacts
a week.

**What this is and isn't.** This is a search-compiled scaffold of ~55 named
communities — enough to start calling Monday. It is *not* the full licensed
roster, which is roughly twice this size. The authoritative list is the NC
DHSR Adult Care Licensure facility listing (PDF/XLSX, with license number,
bed count, and special care unit designation):
**https://info.ncdhhs.gov/dhsr/acls/faclistings.html** — plus the star-rating
search at https://info.ncdhhs.gov/dhsr/acls/star/search.asp. Download the
XLSX, filter to Wake / Durham / Orange, and this doc becomes the complete
ranked sheet. Bed counts and care types below come from directory listings
and should be confirmed on the first call.

CCRCs (Springmoor, Croasdaile, Carol Woods, Carolina Meadows, The Cardinal,
Templeton) stay Tier 2: they fill memory care from their own residents and
mostly don't pay placement fees. Ask, don't assume — and use them as a demand
source: families on their waitlists whose parent needs care now.

## How to rank the calls

| Tier | Who | Why they're first / later |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1 — call first** | Mid-size AL + memory care operators, regional/national chains | They already pay placement fees (to the lead mills), sign standard referral agreements readily, and have census pressure |
| **2 — call second** | CCRCs / life-plan communities | Often waitlisted, sometimes don't pay referral fees at all; needed for range, not revenue |
| **3 — keep in pocket** | Small family care homes (6–12 beds) | Lower rent, fill fast, essential for money-tight (Tier B) families |

On every first call, ask the sales/marketing director three things: current
census, whether they hold placement-agency agreements today (they do — ask
what terms), and which care levels they'll accept. That answers "who's soft"
faster than any directory.

## The six-week sequence

Chains first — one conversation can paper several buildings:

1. **Week 1:** Brookdale corporate (7 Triangle buildings), Carillon (4), Sunrise (4). Ask each for their standard placement-agency agreement and negotiate per the checklist in the growth plan.
2. **Week 2:** Cadence/Cogir (2), Spring Arbor (2), TerraBella (2), Calyx (2), The Addison (2), Avendelle (3 homes).
3. **Weeks 3–4:** Cluster A and B independents (Falls River, Morningside, Waltonwood, Woodland Terrace, Retreat at Cary Corner, Reserve at Mills Farm, Brook Ridge, Senter's, Zebulon House).
4. **Weeks 5–6:** Durham and Orange independents, then CCRCs for range, then small homes for the Tier B safety net.

Done right, weeks 1–2 alone could yield 20+ buildings under agreement.
