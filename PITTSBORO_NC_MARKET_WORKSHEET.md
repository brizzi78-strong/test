## Step 2 Scorecard, Applied

- **Population trend: 5/5.** Chatham County is one of NC's fastest-growing counties; Chatham Park (8,500 acres, ~1,000 residents so far, thousands more planned; South Village's 5,000+ acres approved) will multiply Pittsboro's population.
- **Job market: 4/5.** Wolfspeed's semiconductor plant (2M+ sq ft, $300M+ tax value, $700M+ CHIPS funding, Toyota partnership) plus UNC Health's $15.9M Chatham Park expansion and Research Triangle commuters. Deduct one point: the county terminated its $400M VinFast agreement in July 2026 — don't underwrite deals assuming VinFast jobs materialize.
- **Rent-to-price: 1/5.** At ~0.33% on a median home, buy-and-hold cash flow is deeply negative with financing. Cash flow only works well below the median price point.
- **Days on market: 4/5.** 96 days and climbing; sellers of stale listings are negotiable.
- **Landlord/permit climate: 4/5.** NC is landlord-friendly; watch Chatham County reassessments as values climb.
- **Path of progress: 5/5.** Chatham Park build-out, Wolfspeed, UNC Health, US-64/US-15-501 corridors.

**Score: ~23/30 — a strong appreciation market, a weak cash-flow market.** Strategy fit: flips, land banking in the path of progress, house hacking, or value-add rentals bought well below median. Skip turnkey rentals at market price.

## Where "Undervalued" Hides in a Hot Market

1. **Stale listings (90+ DOM):** With average DOM at 96 days, target listings 120+ days old with price cuts — sellers anchored to 2024–25 peak pricing are capitulating.
2. **Pre-Chatham-Park-era homes:** Older houses in and west of downtown Pittsboro, priced off their dated condition rather than their location, sit in the path of Chatham Park's amenity build-out.
3. **The VinFast disappointment zone (Moncure area):** Sellers and speculators who bought near the VinFast site may be motivated after the county terminated the agreement. Underwrite on today's fundamentals, not revived-plant hopes — but motivated speculators can mean below-market entries near the still-active Wolfspeed corridor.
4. **County spillover:** Siler City (Wolfspeed's doorstep), Goldston, Bear Creek, and Bynum offer lower entry prices with the same county tailwinds; rent ratios improve meaningfully below ~$350K.
5. **Land and teardowns:** In an 8,500-acre master-planned-community town, dirt in the path of progress can be the real undervalued asset.
6. **Tax foreclosures:** Chatham County holds auction-style tax sales in the Justice Center lobby (20% deposit due at the sale); notices run two consecutive weeks in the Chatham News/Record legal ads. Note: unsold parcels become county surplus, and the county currently has a hold on selling surplus property.

## Step 3 Pipeline, Localized

- **On-market:** Saved searches for Pittsboro 27312 + Siler City 27344 + Moncure 27559, sorted by price cuts and DOM; keyword-search "as-is," "estate," "TLC."
- **Tax/foreclosure:** [Chatham County Tax Foreclosure Sales](https://www.chathamcountync.gov/government/departments-programs-i-z/tax-administration/tax-foreclosure-sales) — check notices and the legal ads weekly.
- **Owner/lien research:** [Chatham County Register of Deeds](https://www.chathamcountync.gov/government/departments-programs-i-z/register-of-deeds) (records online, 1771–present), the [Chatham Real Property Data Portal](https://community.spatialest.com/nc/chatham/) for comps and assessments, the [property tax inquiry site](https://chathamnc.devnetwedge.com/search/advanced) for delinquencies, and the county Land Records GIS viewer for parcel/owner lookup.
- **Probate/estate:** Chatham County Clerk of Court estate filings; older housing stock west of downtown skews toward long-tenure owners.
- **Local intel:** Chatham Journal and Chatham EDC announcements move specific corridors — read them like deal flow, because rezonings and site selections show up there before they show up in prices.

## The Numbers Reality Check (Step 5, Local Example)

```
           Max offer = ($600K × 0.70) − $80K rehab = $340K

           needs below-market entry or value-add to clear $150/door
Land bank: Acreage on the US-64 corridor bought from a fatigued
           pre-VinFast speculator — appreciation play, underwrite
           carrying costs with zero income
```

**Bottom line:** In Pittsboro you are hunting mispriced *condition* and *seller fatigue* in the path of documented growth — not cheap cash-flowing houses. Buy the worst house near the newest infrastructure, at a price set by your comps, not the seller's 2025 memories.
