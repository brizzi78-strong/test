# Referral Agency Agreement — template

**Draft for attorney review. Not legal advice.** This is the agreement Cardinal
would *prefer* to sign; most communities will hand you their own form, and the
job is to redline theirs toward these terms (see
[`REFERRAL_AGREEMENTS.md`](REFERRAL_AGREEMENTS.md)). Where a community's form
is silent, these are the clauses to add. Attorney items are marked ⚖.

---

## Referral Agency Agreement

This Referral Agency Agreement (the "Agreement") is made as of ____________,
20__ (the "Effective Date") between **Cardinal Promise Care Bridge LLC**, a
North Carolina limited liability company ("Agency"), and
**______________________________**, the licensed operator of the senior living
community known as ______________________________ located at
______________________________, North Carolina ("Community"). ⚖ *Confirm the
signing entity is the licensed operator, not only the property owner or a
management company; if a management company signs, it must have authority to
bind the operator.*

### 1. Purpose

Agency assists families in identifying senior living options. Community
wishes to receive referrals of prospective residents from Agency and to
compensate Agency for residents who move in as a result. Agency's services to
families are provided at no charge to the family.

### 2. Referral and registration

2.1 **Registration.** A "Referred Prospect" is an individual whose name and
contact information Agency has delivered to Community in writing (email to
the address in Section 11 is sufficient) **before** the individual or the
individual's family first contacts or visits Community, and whom Community
has not already documented as an active prospect within the prior ninety (90)
days. Community will acknowledge each registration in writing within two (2)
business days; failure to object within that period constitutes acceptance
of the registration.

2.2 **Disputed prospects.** If Community contends a registered individual was
already an active prospect, Community will provide its documentation of prior
contact within five (5) business days of the registration. Absent such
documentation, the individual is a Referred Prospect.

2.3 **No obligation.** Nothing obligates Agency to refer any minimum number of
prospects or Community to admit any Referred Prospect. Admission decisions
remain solely with Community.

### 3. Referral fee

3.1 **Amount.** For each Referred Prospect who becomes a resident of Community,
Community shall pay Agency a fee equal to **one hundred percent (100%) of the
resident's total charges for the first full month of residency**, including
base rent or accommodation charges and all care-level, service, and
memory-care charges, but excluding one-time community or move-in fees,
refundable deposits, and pass-through charges for third-party services (the
"Referral Fee").

3.2 **Respite and short stays.** For a Referred Prospect admitted on a respite
or short-term basis who subsequently converts to permanent residency, the
Referral Fee is calculated on the first full month of permanent residency.
⚖ *Some communities pay a reduced fee on respite stays that do not convert;
decide whether to accept.*

3.3 **Attribution period.** The Referral Fee is payable if the Referred
Prospect moves into Community within **twelve (12) months** after
registration, regardless of whether Agency participated in subsequent tours
or communications. ⚖ *Communities commonly propose 90–180 days; six months is
the floor to accept.*

3.4 **Level-of-care changes.** The Referral Fee is calculated at the level of
care and charges in effect on the move-in date, or the first full month
thereafter, whichever is greater.

### 4. Payment

4.1 Community shall pay the Referral Fee within **thirty (30) days** after the
Referred Prospect's move-in date. Agency will invoice at move-in; Community's
obligation does not depend on receipt of an invoice.

4.2 Late payments accrue interest at one percent (1%) per month or the maximum
rate permitted by law, whichever is less. ⚖

4.3 Community shall provide, on request, the resident's first-month statement
sufficient to verify the Referral Fee calculation.

### 5. Refund ("clawback")

5.1 If a Referred Prospect **voluntarily moves out** of Community within
**thirty (30) days** after the move-in date, Agency will refund the Referral
Fee, prorated for days of residency, within thirty (30) days of Community's
written notice.

5.2 **No refund is owed** where the resident's departure results from death,
hospitalization, transfer to a higher level of care (including within
Community's own continuum), Community's discharge or refusal to readmit, or
Community's breach of its residency agreement.

5.3 Community may offset a refund owed under 5.1 only against Referral Fees
due to Agency for the same resident; it may not withhold fees for other
residents.

### 6. Non-exclusivity; independence

6.1 This Agreement is non-exclusive. Agency may refer prospects to any
community, and Community may accept prospects from any source.

6.2 Agency holds no ownership or financial interest in Community. Community
will not offer, and Agency will not accept, any compensation for referrals
other than the Referral Fee described in Section 3.

6.3 **Disclosure to families.** Agency discloses to families, in writing and
before any tour, that participating communities pay Agency a fee upon
move-in, and identifies which communities under discussion do so. Community
acknowledges this disclosure and will not represent Agency's services to any
family as independent of that arrangement. ⚖ *Confirm whether North Carolina
requires any particular form of fee disclosure.*

### 7. Conduct

7.1 Agency will not misrepresent Community's services, availability, pricing,
or care capabilities, and will rely on the information Community provides.

7.2 Community will provide Agency, and keep current, a written statement of
the care levels, conditions, and behaviors Community will and will not accept
(the "Acceptance Criteria," Exhibit A), so that no family is toured at
Community for a resident Community would decline at assessment.

7.3 Neither party will contact a Referred Prospect's family in a manner
inconsistent with the family's stated preferences, and neither will sell or
transfer a family's contact information to any third party.

### 8. Confidentiality and resident information

8.1 Each party will hold the other's non-public business information in
confidence and use it only to perform this Agreement.

8.2 Agency collects only the information needed to identify appropriate
communities and does not create, receive, or maintain clinical records.
Community will not transmit protected health information to Agency beyond
what is necessary to evaluate a referral. ⚖ *Confirm whether a Business
Associate Agreement is appropriate or unnecessary given Agency's role; the
intent is that it is unnecessary.*

### 9. Insurance and compliance

9.1 Agency maintains commercial general liability and professional liability
insurance of not less than $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 aggregate
and will provide a certificate on request.

9.2 Each party will comply with all laws applicable to it. Community
represents it is licensed to operate the Community and will notify Agency of
any change in license status.

### 10. Term and termination

10.1 This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until
terminated by either party on **thirty (30) days'** written notice.

10.2 Termination does not affect Referral Fees for Referred Prospects
registered before the termination date who move in within the attribution
period.

### 11. Notices and registration address

Registrations and notices are effective when sent by email to:

- Community: ______________________________ (name / title / email)
- Agency: registrations@cardinalscarebridge.com ⚖ *(replace with the live
  address)*

### 12. General

Independent contractors; no agency or partnership created. Entire agreement;
amendments in writing. Governed by the laws of North Carolina; venue in Wake
County. ⚖ *Dispute resolution — mediation first, then arbitration or courts;
attorney's preference.* Counterparts and electronic signatures accepted.

__________ Date: ______

### What to expect when redlining a community's form

| Their clause | Your redline |
| --- | --- |
| "75% of first month's base rent" | 100% of total first-month charges incl. care fees (3.1) |
| "90-day refund on any move-out" | 30 days, voluntary only, prorated; death/hospital/discharge excluded (5.1–5.2) |
| "Net 60" | Net 30 (4.1) |
| "Referral valid 90 days" | 12 months, floor 6 (3.3) |
| "Agency shall refer exclusively" / "first right" | Strike (6.1) |
| Silent on registration disputes | Add 2.1–2.2 |
| Silent on acceptance criteria | Add 7.2 and Exhibit A |
| "Agency indemnifies Community for all claims" | Mutual, limited to each party's own negligence ⚖ |

Sign at their standard if they won't move on the rest; hold the line on 3.1
and 5.2, which are the two clauses that decide whether a placement is worth
doing.
