Canva account** as a named design (the earlier candidate links expired;
these do not). For each scene:

1. Use the direct download link (fastest — links expire after a few
   hours; if one is dead, open the design link and Share → Download).
2. Upload the images back in the chat — they get dropped into
   `book/illustrations/pending/` under the target filename and the book
   rebuilds in one command.
3. Don't like one? Open the design link in Canva and regenerate or
   edit it there, or ask for a re-roll with notes.
