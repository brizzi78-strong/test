What CARD is

A fixed-supply ERC-20 token. The entire supply was created once, in a single transaction, when the contract was deployed. The contract has no mint function, so no more can ever exist. After deployment, ownership of the contract was renounced — the owner address is set to zero, which means no person, including its creator, can alter the contract, the supply, or anyone's balance.

There is no roadmap, no development team, no planned features, and no future work. The token is finished. That is the design, not an omission.

What the contract cannot do

These are properties of the deployed code, not intentions. Each is verifiable by reading the source.

  • It cannot create more tokens. No mint function exists.
  • It cannot destroy or move your tokens. No burn, no clawback, no admin transfer.
  • It cannot tax your transfers. A transfer of any amount moves exactly that amount.
  • It cannot freeze or block an address. No blocklist exists.
  • It cannot be paused. No pause function exists.
  • It cannot be upgraded or changed. The code is immutable.

Where the supply is

80% of the supply was placed into a Uniswap V2 liquidity pool. The liquidity provider tokens are locked, with the locker and expiry published above.

The remaining 20% is held in the treasury address published above. The treasury will never be sold. It is donated to registered nonprofit organizations on a fixed schedule, published in advance.
