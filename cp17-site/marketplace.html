A preview, not a promise

The marketplace.

What we hope CARD grows into: a small marketplace where the coin can be used with real, participating businesses — the kinds of places that carry families through hard seasons. This page shows the idea. It does not pretend the idea is finished.

Read this part first. Nothing on this page exists yet. No business has signed up, nothing is for sale here, and CARD itself has not launched. This marketplace becomes real only if real businesses choose to join after launch — and when one does, it will be listed here with proof, the same way everything else on this site works. Until then, treat this page as a sketch.

How it would work

01 Hold some CARD

The coin sits in your own wallet, the way the how-to-buy page describes. No account with us, no sign-up.

02 Pick a participating business

Businesses that choose to accept CARD would be listed here — by name, with their address and their own say-so you could verify directly with them. Only real, signed-up businesses would ever appear. No business is listed today, because none has signed up.

03 Pay them directly

You'd send CARD from your wallet to theirs, directly, the way any wallet-to-wallet payment works. We would not sit in the middle or hold anyone's money. One thing does apply: the coin's built-in 2% fee. It is charged on every transfer between ordinary wallets, so 2% of any payment goes to the treasury and the business receives 98%. That is written into the contract and we cannot waive it.

Who we hope joins

The point was never coffee shops. The kinds of businesses we'd knock on doors for are the ones that carried this family:

  • Caregiving Home health, hospice, respite care — the people who show up when a family is at its most tired.
  • Recovery Treatment and support for addiction and mental illness. There is a story behind this coin because of work like theirs.
  • Community life The local places that hold a neighborhood together — the ones that know your name before your order.

These are categories, not commitments. No organization named anywhere on this site has signed up for, endorsed, or even heard of this marketplace.

If you run a business

If this ever becomes real, it starts with a conversation, not a form. After launch, this page will say plainly how to reach us. If you're reading this before then: watch this page — every real step will be posted here first, like everything else.

A marketplace with zero merchants is just a promise with a nicer name. So this page holds itself to the site's rule: nothing appears here before it has actually happened.
