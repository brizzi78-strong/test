How to buy HOP

We don't recommend buying HOP, and that isn't fine print — it's our honest advice. But if you understand the risk and want to anyway, here are the steps, written for someone who has never bought a cryptocurrency and doesn't want to pretend otherwise. No jargon. About 20 minutes the first time.

Understand the risk before you start. HOP is a souvenir, not an investment, and I will never pitch it as one. The price can go to nothing. The founder's own holding is unlocked, and the pool behind the coin is small — both are spelled out on the coin page, and you should read that page before this one. If you take part, use money you'd be at peace never seeing again — the way you'd treat something you gave away, not something you're counting on. Please don't contribute money you can't live without.

HOP has not launched yet. When it does, the official address will appear on the coin page — and nowhere else.

What you'll need

Three things: a little Ethereum (ETH), a wallet app, and the official HOP address. The steps below get you all three.

Step 1 — Buy some Ethereum (10 minutes)

ETH is the money you trade for HOP — like changing dollars into euros before a trip. HOP itself only trades on Uniswap; the ETH just gets you there. Robinhood is the cheapest easy place to buy it.

In the Robinhood app, find Ethereum (ETH). Buy the amount you decided on, plus a few extra dollars for the network fee. You may be asked for a photo ID — that's normal and required by law.

Want quicker instead of cheaper? Coinbase works the same way — faster to set up, higher fees. The wallet app in Step 2 also has a Buy button that takes a debit card.

Step 2 — Get a wallet app (5 minutes)

Download Rainbow or MetaMask — free apps that hold your coins. Use those links or your phone's app store, nothing else; fake wallet apps exist.

The app will show you 12 random words. They are the only key to your money. Write them on paper, keep it safe, and never give them to anyone — not support, not us, not ever.

Step 3 — Send your ETH to your wallet (5 minutes)

In your wallet, tap Receive and copy your address. In Robinhood or Coinbase, open your ETH, tap Send, and paste it. Always paste, never type. The first time, Robinhood walks you through turning on crypto transfers — be patient.

Nervous? Send a few dollars first, watch it arrive, then send the rest.

Step 4 — Trade the ETH for HOP (5 minutes)

Open app.uniswap.org in your wallet's browser. It's an open exchange — no account needed. Check the address bar says app.uniswap.org before you trade.

  1. Set the top box to ETH and type how much you want to spend.
  2. In the bottom box, paste the official HOP address from the coin page. Copy and paste it — never type it by hand.
  3. Check that the name shown is Hope Coin (HOP), then confirm.

About a minute later, the HOP is in your wallet. Every transfer costs 2% — built into the coin, paid to the public treasury — plus Uniswap's 0.3%. There's no penalty for selling whenever you want.

The good news: you only do this once. The first three steps are one-time setup. After that, the Buy button on the front page opens Uniswap with HOP already selected — buying again is two taps.

If this feels too hard, that's not you failing. Stop. Nobody needs to own one of these. These apps were built by people who have never watched someone patiently try to do the right thing and get nowhere — the difficulty is theirs, not yours. Put the phone down. You've lost nothing.

Staying safe

  • One address, ever. Scammers create fake coins using real names. If an address didn't come from the coin page, it isn't ours.
  • Nobody will ever message you first. Not us, not "support," not an admin. Anyone who direct-messages you about HOP is trying to take your money.
  • Your 12 words are never needed. Not to buy, not to fix a problem, not to claim anything. A request for them is always theft.
  • Nothing is free. Giveaways, airdrops, "send 1 get 2 back" — all fake, without exception.
