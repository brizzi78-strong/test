How to buy CARD

Written for someone who has never bought a cryptocurrency and doesn't want to pretend otherwise. No jargon. About 20 minutes the first time.

Please don't contribute money you can't live without. I mean that as plainly as I can say it. CARD is not an investment, and I will never pitch it as one. The price can go to nothing. If you take part, use money you'd be at peace never seeing again — the way you'd treat something you gave away, not something you're counting on.

CARD has not launched yet. When it does, the official address will appear on the coin page — and nowhere else.

What you'll need

Three things: an app called a wallet, a small amount of a currency called Ethereum (ETH), and the official CARD address. That's it. The steps below get you all three.

01 Get a wallet app 5 minutes

Download Rainbow or MetaMask from your phone's app store. Both are free, and both do the same job: hold your coins and let you trade them.

When you set it up, it shows you a list of 12 random words. That list is the only key to your money. Write it on paper. Keep it somewhere safe. Never photograph it, never email it, never type it into a website, and never give it to a human being for any reason — including someone claiming to be support, or us.

02 Put some ETH in it 10 minutes

ETH is the currency you trade for CARD, the way you'd change dollars into euros before buying something abroad.

The wallet app has a Buy button. Tap it, use a debit card, and buy a small amount of ETH — whatever you decided you were comfortable spending, plus a few extra dollars. Those extra dollars cover the network's transaction fee, which everyone pays and which changes by the hour.

The first purchase may ask you to verify your identity with a photo ID. That's normal and required by law.

03 Trade the ETH for CARD 5 minutes

Tap Swap inside your wallet app, or open app.uniswap.org in the app's browser. Uniswap is not a company you sign up with — it's an open exchange anyone can use.

  1. Set the top box to ETH and type how much you want to spend.
  2. In the bottom box, paste the official CARD address from the coin page. Copy and paste it — never type it by hand.
  3. Check that the name shown is Cardinals Promise (CARD), then confirm.

Within about a minute, the CARD appears in your wallet. Every trade costs 0.3%, charged by Uniswap on every coin traded there. There is no extra fee from us, and no penalty for selling whenever you want.

If this feels too hard, that's not you failing. Stop. Nobody needs to own one of these. These apps were built by people who have never watched someone patiently try to do the right thing and get nowhere — the difficulty is theirs, not yours. Put the phone down. You've lost nothing.

Staying safe

  • One address, ever. Scammers create fake coins using real names. If an address didn't come from the coin page, it isn't ours.
  • Nobody will ever message you first. Not us, not "support," not an admin. Anyone who direct-messages you about CARD is trying to take your money.
  • Your 12 words are never needed. Not to buy, not to fix a problem, not to claim anything. A request for them is always theft.
  • Nothing is free. Giveaways, airdrops, "send 1 get 2 back" — all fake, without exception.
