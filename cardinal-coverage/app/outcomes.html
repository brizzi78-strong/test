Period

How the metrics are computed. On-time rate counts authorization submissions logged on time against all submissions logged. Appeal win rate counts decided appeals only — pending appeals are excluded from both numerator and denominator, so the rate is not diluted by open cases. Covered days recovered and dollars protected sum only won-appeal and recovered-days events; the fields are disabled on every other event type. Pending appeals are counted per case across all events, not just the selected window, and a win rate is withheld below five decided appeals. Future-dated or unreadable entries are excluded from every total and flagged.

This is a working measurement aid, not an accounting system. Before any figure is used in an investor conversation or a price negotiation, reconcile it against remittance advice and your billing system.
