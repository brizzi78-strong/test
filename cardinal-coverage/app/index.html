Cardinal Coverage

Know every covered day. Keep every earned dollar.

Working prototypes for managing Medicare Advantage authorizations from admission through discharge. This area is private — it is password-gated and marked no-index.

Daily tools

Authorization tracker

Every resident's covered days, next submission, NOMNC deliver-by, QIO fast-track deadline, recertification and progress note — sorted most urgent first.

Appeal letters

Fast-track QIO appeal, plan reconsideration, and short-authorization challenge, assembled around your clinical record.

Outcomes log

Thirty seconds per event. Builds the recovered-days and protected-dollars figure that sets pricing.

Plan rules registry

What each payer requires, with the source, who confirmed it, and when it expires.

Investor materials — not for public distribution

Presentation deck

Sixteen slides. Arrow keys to advance, N for speaker notes, P to print. Still contains FILL IN blocks.
