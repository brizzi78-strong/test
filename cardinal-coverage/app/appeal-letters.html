Have counsel review before first use. An independent audit flagged two issues these drafts do not yet resolve. Standing: parties to a plan reconsideration under 42 CFR 422.574 are the enrollee, an appointed representative (Form CMS-1696), or a non-contracted provider acting as assignee with a signed waiver of liability — a facility signature alone may be dismissed without reaching the merits. Expedited handling: under 42 CFR 422.584(c)(2) the plan must expedite when a physician indicates the standard is met, and may decline when an enrollee asks; an "expedited review requested" header over a nursing signature secures nothing.
